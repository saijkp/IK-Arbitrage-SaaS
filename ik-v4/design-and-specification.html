Design & specification

Interior design and furniture specification for rental property

No designer? Our in-house designer can plan the rooms, choose finishes and prepare a full product schedule, ready for us to source.

Who it's for

Homeowners, landlords and developers who don't have a designer, and want one clear plan before anything is ordered.

  • Homeowners furnishing a whole house or several rooms at once
  • Landlords fitting out HMOs, serviced apartments or holiday lets
  • Developers who need one specification repeated across units

Already working with a designer? You don't need this service. See below.

What's included

  1. A short design call or questionnaire about the property, your budget and the style you want.
  2. Room layouts for the agreed rooms: furniture placement, kitchen and wardrobe layouts.
  3. A mood board with finishes and colours.
  4. A full product schedule ready to source: items, sizes, finishes and quantities.
  5. Two rounds of changes. Further rounds are quoted separately.
  6. Written sign-off of the product schedule before anything is ordered.

What a product schedule contains

A useful schedule gives each item a room and product reference, so factory options, approvals and delivery checks refer to the same thing. Record dimensions and finishes rather than relying on a photograph. List a mattress, bed frame and upholstered headboard separately, with the evidence required for each.

Designing for HMOs and rental property

Choose finishes with everyday cleaning and maintenance in mind. Ask for care instructions, samples and details of replaceable parts before approving surfaces and upholstery. Check exposed edges, handles and fixings as well as colour: these are frequent contact points in occupied rooms.

Plan storage for each bedroom rather than dividing a total wardrobe quantity across the house. Measure hanging space, shelves and drawers alongside the bed and desk, and check that doors and drawers can open within the layout. Record any room-specific differences in the schedule.

Repeating one specification across rooms or units makes replacements and order checks easier. Use common item references and finishes, with a separate quantity for each room type. Confirm the fit in each layout before repeating it; a smaller room may need a different wardrobe or bed size.

For upholstered furniture, ask for permanent fire-label information and product-specific compliance evidence. List mattresses and bed bases separately and request the evidence relevant to their intended use; sofa labels and mattress evidence are not interchangeable. Keep label photographs and documents against the item reference. Our HMO furniture fire-safety guide explains what to ask suppliers for and how to keep the records together.

Serviced apartments and holiday lets

Plan furniture around guest numbers, luggage storage and cleaning between stays. Record sofa-bed dimensions both open and closed, dining places and spare bedding storage in the room schedule. For the wider package, see our serviced apartment furnishing service and room-by-room guide to furnishing a serviced apartment from China.

What's not included

Building work, structural or planning drawings, site management, or styling visits, unless agreed in writing.

How it works

  1. Brief. Tell us about the property, the rooms, your budget and your timing.
  2. Design. Our designer prepares the layouts, mood board and product schedule.
  3. Revisions. You review the design and ask for changes, with two rounds included.
  4. Sign-off. You approve the product schedule in writing.
  5. Factory options and pricing. We come back with factory options and pricing within 48 hours of the signed-off schedule.
  6. Production. Once you approve the quotation, production starts. Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings.
  7. Delivery. We take care of shipping, customs clearance and delivery to your property.

The fee

Design & specification is charged as a fixed fee agreed before we start, credited against your order if you go ahead. Product sourcing is quoted as usual, with our 5% sourcing fee shown on every quotation.

Already have a designer? We work to your designer's specification and drawings at no design fee, and we never approach their clients for design work. See our sourcing package for design studios.

Frequently asked questions

How much does design & specification cost?

It's a fixed fee agreed before we start, so you know the cost up front. The fee is credited against your order if you go ahead.

How many changes can I make?

Two rounds of changes are included. Further rounds are quoted separately.

Do I have to order through you?

No. The design fee is agreed on its own. If you go ahead with your order through us, the fee is credited against it.

What if I already have a designer?

Then you don't need this service. We work to your designer's specification and drawings at no design fee, and we never approach their clients for design work.

How long does the design stage take?

It depends on the number of rooms and how quickly decisions are made. We agree the timing when we confirm the scope.

Can you design one specification for several properties?

Yes. We can prepare one specification to repeat across rooms or units, with quantities and any layout differences recorded for each property. We agree the scope before starting.

Do you design kitchens and wardrobes?

Yes. Kitchen and wardrobe layouts are included for the agreed rooms. Building work, structural or planning drawings, site management and styling visits are excluded unless agreed in writing.
