Design & specification

Design & Specification for Your Furnishing Package

No designer? Our in-house designer can plan the rooms, choose finishes and prepare a full product schedule, ready for us to source.

Who it's for

Homeowners, landlords and developers who don't have a designer, and want one clear plan before anything is ordered.

  • Homeowners furnishing a whole house or several rooms at once
  • Landlords fitting out HMOs, serviced apartments or holiday lets
  • Developers who need one specification repeated across units

Already working with a designer? You don't need this service. See below.

What's included

  1. A short design call or questionnaire about the property, your budget and the style you want.
  2. Room layouts for the agreed rooms: furniture placement, kitchen and wardrobe layouts.
  3. A mood board with finishes and colours.
  4. A full product schedule ready to source: items, sizes, finishes and quantities.
  5. Two rounds of changes. Further rounds are quoted separately.
  6. Written sign-off of the product schedule before anything is ordered.

What's not included

Building work, structural or planning drawings, site management, or styling visits, unless agreed in writing.

How it works

  1. Brief. Tell us about the property, the rooms, your budget and your timing.
  2. Design. Our designer prepares the layouts, mood board and product schedule.
  3. Revisions. You review the design and ask for changes, with two rounds included.
  4. Sign-off. You approve the product schedule in writing.
  5. Factory options and pricing. We come back with factory options and pricing within 48 hours of the signed-off schedule.
  6. Production. Once you approve the quotation, production starts. Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings.
  7. Delivery. We manage shipping and delivery to your property, with customs handled by an authorised UK broker.

The fee

Design & specification is charged as a fixed fee agreed before we start, credited against your order if you go ahead. Product sourcing is quoted as usual, with our 5% sourcing fee shown on every quotation.

Already have a designer? We work to your designer's specification and drawings at no design fee, and we never approach their clients for design work. See our sourcing package for design studios.

Frequently asked questions

How much does design & specification cost?

It's a fixed fee agreed before we start, so you know the cost up front. The fee is credited against your order if you go ahead.

How many changes can I make?

Two rounds of changes are included. Further rounds are quoted separately.

Do I have to order through you?

No. The design fee is agreed on its own. If you go ahead with your order through us, the fee is credited against it.

What if I already have a designer?

Then you don't need this service. We work to your designer's specification and drawings at no design fee, and we never approach their clients for design work.

How long does the design stage take?

It depends on the number of rooms and how quickly decisions are made. We agree the timing when we confirm the scope.

Want help with the design?

Send your floor plans or a room list, your budget and your timing, and tick "I'd like help with design & specification". We'll confirm the scope and the fixed fee before we start.
