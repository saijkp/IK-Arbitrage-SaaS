Whole house furniture package

Furnish an Entire House in One Order — Not Room by Room

Every room specified, quoted and delivered together as a single package, sourced from China — instead of assembling a house piecemeal from several UK retailers at retail markup.

The problem

Furnishing a whole property from scratch usually means becoming your own project manager: one retailer for the bedrooms, another for the living room, a third for the kitchen, each with its own delivery window, its own quality variance, and its own markup. Styles don't always match, delivery dates rarely line up, and there's no single point of contact if something's wrong when it arrives.

How a whole house package works

  • One brief covering every room — bedrooms, living room, kitchen, bathroom furniture, whatever the property needs
  • Specified and quoted as a single package, not room-by-room separate orders
  • One factory-confirmed order, inspected before shipping with a full photo report before the container seals
  • One consolidated delivery to the property, on a confirmed date
  • All-in landed pricing — product, freight, customs clearance, duty and delivery — so there's one number, not a dozen invoices
