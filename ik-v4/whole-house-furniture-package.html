Whole house furniture package

Furnish an Entire House in One Order — Not Room by Room

Every room specified, quoted and delivered together as a single package, sourced from China — instead of assembling a house piecemeal from several UK retailers at retail markup.

The problem

Furnishing a whole property from scratch usually means becoming your own project manager: one retailer for the bedrooms, another for the living room, a third for the kitchen, each with its own delivery window, its own quality variance, and its own markup. Styles don't always match, delivery dates rarely line up, and there's no single point of contact if something's wrong when it arrives.

How a whole house package works

  • One brief covering every room — bedrooms, living room, kitchen, bathroom furniture, whatever the property needs
  • Specified and quoted as a single package, not room-by-room separate orders
  • One factory-confirmed order, inspected before shipping with a full photo report before the container seals
  • One consolidated delivery to the property, on a confirmed date
  • A compliance pack with BS 7177 evidence for mattresses and beds, permanent fire-label details for relevant upholstered items, and UKCA documents for electrical goods.

Planning a whole house room by room

Most whole-house projects start with the kitchen and utility, bedrooms and dressing areas, living and dining rooms, study and lighting. Curtains and blinds, bathroom furniture and flooring can then be added as clearly defined packages. Reference photos set the style; measured plans turn that style into a schedule we can price.

Budgeting for a whole house

We can build options at different budget levels for the same house. A house can't be priced reliably from floor area or photographs alone, so every quotation states whether fitted joinery, bathrooms, flooring, delivery and installation are included. For early planning figures per room, see our budget planning guide.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

What should whole house furniture packages include?

Start with a room-by-room list and mark each item as essential, optional or already owned. Include bed and mattress sizes, seating capacity, dining places, storage and window measurements. Separate freestanding furniture from fitted kitchens, wardrobes, lighting and flooring so each trade can confirm its part.

Agree one palette, then approve material samples and dimensions before ordering. Confirm the delivery route, assembly responsibilities and which rooms must be ready first. Explore kitchen cabinet sourcing and tiles and flooring sourcing when building the full schedule.

No designer? Our in-house designer can plan the rooms, choose finishes and prepare a full product schedule, ready for us to source. It's a fixed fee agreed before we start, credited against your order if you go ahead. See our design and specification service.
