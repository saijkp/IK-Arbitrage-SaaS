Buying an Aluminium Pergola from China: What to Check

Put the pergola’s dimensions, materials, load documentation and delivery arrangements into a written specification before paying a deposit. Review factory evidence against your site with the installer or designer, and resolve documentation and customs questions before production.

Specify alloy and wall thickness

Ask for the aluminium alloy and temper, profile drawings and wall thickness for posts, beams and blades separately. Agree tolerances and inspection points, including joints and reinforcement. A product photograph or total weight does not establish the profile specification.

Record the powder-coat specification

Confirm powder brand and product reference, coating thickness, surface preparation, RAL colour and gloss level. Approve a physical finish sample and ask for care instructions and warranty exclusions for the site’s exposure. Record how coating thickness will be checked at inspection.

Check factory wind and snow figures against the site

Request the figures and supporting reports for the actual span, frame configuration, roof and screens quoted. Ask a competent designer to compare them with site exposure, foundations and fixings. Confirm operating positions and weather limitations in writing; a generic brochure figure cannot approve the installation.

Trace drainage through the posts

Ask for drawings showing blade channels, gutters, post drainage and outlet positions. Agree how outlets meet the site drainage and how gutters can be cleaned. Include agreed water-flow checks in the pre-shipment inspection and confirm installation falls with the installer.

Request motor and control documentation

Identify motor, controller, sensors and lighting by exact model. Request relevant UKCA or CE marking documentation, wiring diagrams and English manuals, with control and power-loss instructions. We check the documentation is supplied; we do not certify products. Have the electrician and installer review it before approving production.

Specify fixings and packing

Confirm the stainless steel grade and suitability of fixings for the environment with the designer, including how dissimilar metals are isolated. Long profiles need supported crates, protected ends and finish protection. Request crate lengths, weights, lifting points, component labels and a packing list before booking freight.

Compare LCL and FCL using crate dimensions

Shared-container LCL can suit smaller consignments; full-container FCL may suit a larger or combined order. Compare handling, long-crate acceptance, packed volume and destination charges using the LCL vs FCL shipping guide. Confirm delivery vehicle access and who unloads the crates.

Confirm classification and trade-remedy scope

Agree the HS code and full UK commodity code with the customs broker using the exact goods and shipment configuration. Our UK customs clearance guide lists the paperwork to prepare. Anti-dumping measures exist for certain aluminium extrusions from China; scope depends on the exact product, and we check at quotation. See the official aluminium extrusions trade-remedy notice. Do not infer the treatment of your order from the material name alone.

Agree inspection, spares and payment

Inspect against approved drawings, finish samples, component lists and agreed functional checks before shipping. Record spare motor, controller, seal and fixing references and the warranty process. Confirm planning and compare roof types before final approval.

50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading. Shipping, duty and VAT are invoiced separately.

Frequently asked questions

What profile details should be on the order?

Record alloy, temper, profile drawings and wall thickness for posts, beams and blades, with agreed tolerances and inspection points.

Do factory load figures approve my site?

No. Ask a competent designer to review the evidence for the exact model against your site, foundations, anchoring and intended use.

Does IK ENG certify motors and controls?

No. We check that the requested UKCA or CE documentation, wiring diagrams and English manuals are supplied for professional review.

Can I assume the customs treatment from the word aluminium?

No. Classification and trade-remedy scope depend on the exact product and shipment configuration. We check at quotation with the relevant product information.

What are the payment terms?

50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading. Shipping, duty and VAT are invoiced separately.
