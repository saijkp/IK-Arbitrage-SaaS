Student accommodation furniture cost per bed

Student accommodation furniture cost per bed is useful only when the furniture list and shared costs are clear. No per-bed price is given here. Build the allowance from approved room types, then add communal furniture, logistics and installation visibly.

Define the cost-per-bed framework

Give each room type a code and record the number of rooms and occupied bed spaces it represents. Separate standard rooms, studios, accessible layouts and retained furniture. For each type, sum the specified bedroom items, then allocate its agreed share of communal furniture. Keep the allocation basis beside the calculation so a studio and a cluster bedroom are not treated as the same scope.

Use: bedroom furniture subtotal + allocated communal furniture share = goods allowance per bed. Show fee, freight, taxes, delivery, installation, samples and spares separately before calculating a delivered allowance. Record whether the denominator is furnished bed spaces or rooms, and avoid dividing by forecast occupancy.

No student per-bed figure is given. Published indicative figures are available in HMO furnishing cost planning and hotel bedroom furniture costs. These have different contents and exclusions; do not relabel them as student room prices.

Use a room-type schedule and approve a model room

For every item record code, quantity by room type, dimensions, material, finish, hardware, evidence required and revision. Show doors, sockets, radiators and circulation on the plans. Reconcile the total order with the room-type counts and keep shared furniture in its own schedule. The student furniture specification checklist provides a starting template.

Assemble the proposed bed, mattress, desk, chair and storage as a model room before approving repetition. Check comfort, access, cleaning, finish and fixing details with the operator and installer. Sample/model-room costs are confirmed in writing before you pay. Record amendments and obtain approval of the final schedule before bulk manufacture.

Construction and compliance evidence

Specify contract-grade construction with 18 mm boards and commercial hardware where agreed; check it at inspection against the schedule and model room. Record replaceable handles, hinges, runners and edge details rather than relying on the phrase “contract grade”.

Compliance is confirmed per order from factory test evidence: BS 7177 for beds/mattresses, permanent fire labels BS 5852 / BS EN 1021 for relevant upholstered items, and UKCA on electrical goods. Match evidence to the exact product and construction, and agree suitability for the intended setting with the project adviser before approval. The Ireland hotel guest-room furniture and lighting case study offers related repeated-room sourcing context; it is not a student cost benchmark.

Plan spares and academic-year delivery windows

Choose spare stock by item, repair history, available storage and the impact of a missing component. List handles, hinges, glides, drawer parts and matching finishes alongside any complete replacement furniture. Do not use an unsupported blanket percentage. Label spares separately so installers do not consume them as room stock.

Work backwards from the operator’s actual resident departure and arrival dates. Confirm access after departures, other trades, cleaning, delivery, assembly, snagging and handover before reoccupation. Academic calendars and site access vary; agree the delivery window with each institution or operator rather than assuming a universal summer programme.

We consolidate at our warehouse in China. Use building, floor, cluster, room and item codes on carton labels and the packing list. Confirm unloading, lift access, carrying routes and staged storage. Keep a delivery reconciliation and snag list by room so shortages remain visible before residents arrive.

Keep the delivered budget itemised

List product value, the IK ENG sourcing fee of 5% on product value, samples, inspection, freight, insurance, customs clearance, duty, import VAT, UK delivery and installation separately. Ask which charges are included, estimated or excluded, and who pays each supplier. Payment is 50% deposit / 50% after pre-shipment inspection before loading. Confirm logistics payment dates in the written quotation.

There is no minimum to enquire; factory MOQs vary by product. Sample/model-room costs are confirmed in writing before you pay. Our own team can carry in, assemble and install as a separately quoted line, or you can use your own fitters. Confirm the handover between furniture assembly and specialist UK trades.

Frequently asked questions

What is student accommodation furniture cost per bed?

It is a defined bedroom furniture allowance plus the allocated communal furniture share for each bed space. No per-bed price is given here; use an itemised schedule and separate delivered costs.

What should the bedroom allowance include?

List the bed, mattress, desk, chair, wardrobe or storage, shelving, pinboard and task lighting. Identify integrated items and retained furniture to avoid double counting.

How should communal furniture be allocated?

Price the communal schedule separately and divide its agreed scope by the bed spaces it serves. Keep the allocation basis and exclusions visible.

Is a model room included free?

Sample/model-room costs are confirmed in writing before you pay. Agree the scope and approvals before committing to repeat rooms.

When should student furniture be delivered?

Agree delivery around the operator’s actual departure, access and arrival dates, allowing for assembly, cleaning, snagging and handover. Do not assume a universal academic-year delivery window.
