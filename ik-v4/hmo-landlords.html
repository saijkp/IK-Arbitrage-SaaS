How it works for HMO landlords

  1. Record each room. Send measured plans, the bedroom inventory, shared-area quantities, postcode, budget and required date. Mark retained furniture and optional items separately.
  2. Check layouts and evidence. Confirm bed and mattress compatibility, storage access and delivery routes. List product-specific mattress and upholstery evidence against each item; use the HMO furniture fire-safety guide to prepare the document requests.
  3. Approve the order. Compare factory options against the same inventory, then approve dimensions, finishes, samples where needed and the itemised quotation before production.
  4. Inspect and allocate. Check the finished goods against the approved schedule and keep the photo report, product references and supporting documents with the property inventory.
  5. Prepare for tenant move-in. Agree keys, parking, unloading, the receiving contact and the delivery point. Allow for assembly and room checks after delivery before tenants arrive.

HMO landlords FAQs

What should I send for an HMO furniture quotation?

Send measured plans, a room-by-room inventory, shared-area quantities, delivery postcode, budget and required date. Identify retained items and access restrictions so the quotation covers the actual refurbishment.

Are kitchens and shared areas included in a bedroom pack?

They need separate schedules and quotation lines. List shared seating, dining furniture, kitchen cabinets, bathroom products and lighting alongside the bedrooms so none is assumed within a bedroom allowance.

What fire-safety evidence should I keep with the inventory?

Request product-specific mattress and bed evidence, applicable permanent fire-label details for upholstered furniture, and the supporting documents for the actual products supplied. Keep these with approved references and delivery records.

Can you help with furniture replacements between tenancies?

Keep item references, finishes and spare-part details for replacement enquiries. Check urgent items separately; UK stock may suit an immediate replacement better than a new China sourcing order.

Got rooms to furnish?

Send your plans, schedule or reference images, delivery postcode, approximate budget and required timing. We'll come back within 48 hours with factory options and pricing.
