What is in an HMO furniture package?

A bedroom pack can include a bed frame, mattress, wardrobe, bedside units and a desk or chair where the layout allows. Shared living and dining furniture is scheduled separately. Check bed sizes, usable storage, door swings and the route upstairs before approving quantities. Our HMO furnishing cost guide explains goods-only room budgets and the costs to add.

HMO furniture suppliers: check the fire-safety paperwork

Specify BS 7177 for mattresses and beds and permanent fire labels on relevant upholstered items. Keep product references, test certificates and delivery records in your landlord file. Our compliance pack brings the documents together. Read the HMO furniture fire-safety guide for the distinction between upholstery labels and mattress evidence.

Landlord furniture in Manchester, delivered across the UK

From our base in Salford, we help Manchester landlords and property owners across the UK plan room-by-room orders. For an empty property, confirm a named receiver, parking, keys, floor access and dry storage. Agree whether the delivery ends at the kerb, entrance or room before booking trades and tenant move-in dates.

Replacing furniture between tenancies

Keep a room inventory and product references so replacements can match the original specification. Order spare handles and other agreed parts with the main package where practical. A single urgent replacement may be better bought from UK stock: China sourcing suits planned refurbishment and repeat orders, with standard items typically taking 10–12 weeks to site and bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings.
