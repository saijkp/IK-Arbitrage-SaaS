Buying a Sauna from China: What to Check

Put the sauna specification, documentation and delivery arrangements in writing before paying a deposit. A listing photograph cannot confirm timber grade, heater suitability or what will arrive in the crate, so use an agreed checklist for the order and inspection.

Confirm the timber and construction in writing

Specify timber species and grade for the walls, benches, floor and external parts separately. Record wall or board thickness and whether the cabin is solid timber or framed and insulated. Ask for drawings showing joints, supports, fixings, ventilation and bench dimensions.

Agree acceptable knots and cracks with reference photographs, including limits for areas people touch. Ask how moisture content is measured and what range the supplier commits to at inspection and packing. A timber name alone does not define its grade, condition or finish.

Request heater and controller documentation

Confirm the exact heater, controller and sensors against cabin volume and your installer's supply assessment. Request UKCA or CE marking information, relevant conformity documents, English manuals, wiring diagrams and control panel documentation before approving the order.

IK ENG checks that this documentation is supplied; it does not certify products. Send the pack to your electrician for review and use our UK sauna electrical requirements guide to prepare the questions.

Check the glass, door and outdoor roof

Record glass type, thickness and dimensions, with the specified safety glazing documentation. Check the door opening, hinges, handle, seals and the clearances required by the design. The drawing should show how the door operates and how glass is protected in transit.

For outdoor units, specify the roof covering, flashing, fixings and weatherproof joints. Confirm whether shingles, sheet roofing and trims are supplied or excluded. Review the base and drainage requirements, and check planning requirements with your council before approving external dimensions.

Agree packing and pre-shipment inspection

Long panels and staves need support, protected ends and a packing method that limits movement during handling. Ask for crate dimensions and weight, a component list, moisture protection arrangements and separate protection for glass, heater and controls.

Agree an inspection checklist against the signed specification: timber condition, dimensions, panel fit, bench and door operation, component count, documentation and packing. Define any assembly or functional checks in advance. Our pre-shipment inspection and factory visits service records the agreed checks; inspection does not replace installation checks or product conformity obligations.

Compare LCL and FCL using packed dimensions

Use the packed volume, weight and longest crate to compare shared-container LCL and full-container FCL shipping. LCL involves shared cargo handling; FCL may suit a larger order or a consignment combined with other goods. Confirm handling restrictions and destination delivery arrangements rather than choosing from the unpacked cabin size. See our LCL vs FCL guide.

Confirm customs classification before committing

Duty depends on classification. A prefabricated timber building, a separately supplied sauna heater and other equipment may require different commodity codes and treatment. Do not apply one assumed rate to every sauna order.

Confirm the HS code and the full UK commodity code with your customs broker using the actual construction, components and shipment details before you commit. Use the UK Trade Tariff and our UK customs clearance guide to prepare the paperwork.

Plan the final delivery and UK support

Check pallet or crate access from the delivery vehicle to the unloading point and onward to the base. Record gate widths, turns, slopes, overhead clearance and ground conditions. Agree who provides unloading or lifting equipment and whether delivery is to the kerb or an agreed site position.

Get warranty terms in writing, including commercial use if relevant, who handles a UK claim, what labour or transport is excluded and how replacement parts are obtained. Ask about spare door hardware, controllers, sensors and matching timber, with part references and availability confirmed for the chosen model.

Turn an Alibaba listing into an order specification

A listing can start the discussion, but confirm the exact model, included items, drawings and packing rather than assuming the photographs form the contract. Our Alibaba-to-UK shipping guide covers supplier and freight information to collect. Send the listing with your dimensions and delivery postcode through our sauna sourcing page.

Frequently asked questions

What timber details should be on the order?

Confirm the species, grade and thickness for each part in writing, with agreed acceptance criteria for knots, cracks and moisture condition. Include construction drawings and finish details.

Does a marking on the heater replace documentation checks?

No. Request the relevant UKCA or CE documentation, English manuals, wiring diagrams and control panel documentation for the exact model. IK ENG checks that documentation is supplied and does not certify products.

Is the duty rate the same for every sauna shipment?

No. Classification depends on the goods, including whether they are a prefabricated timber building, a separate heater or other equipment. Confirm the HS code and full UK commodity code with your customs broker before committing.

When is the balance payable?

IK ENG works on 50% deposit to place the order and 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading.
