When China may not be the right choice

Check your timescale first. If you need a kitchen in a few weeks, or a single replacement unit, a UK supplier will usually suit you better. China works best when the layout is measured, the specification is settled and the order allows for manufacture and shipping. Our focus is the right material, finish and quality for the budget, not simply the lowest price.

How to compare kitchen quotes properly

We compare carcass and back-board grade, door construction and finish, hardware models, drawer counts and every finishing panel. A room length is not a cabinet schedule: to quote accurately we need measured walls, ceiling heights, window and door openings, service positions and your appliance list.

Plywood, moisture-resistant MDF or MFC?

We can price comparable plywood, moisture-resistant MDF and particleboard (MFC) carcass options for the same layout. A cheaper quote often reflects a different board, door finish, hardware allowance or a missing panel schedule — so the choice is made against the actual specification, not the headline price.

Making a Chinese-made kitchen fit a UK home

Factories make to many markets, so UK details must be written into the schedule rather than assumed.

  • Appliances: confirm exact model numbers, then check housing sizes, ventilation and integrated door fixings against each manufacturer's drawings.
  • Heights: agree the plinth, worktop and tall-unit heights against your ceiling and any uneven floors.
  • Services: mark sockets, water, waste, gas and extraction so cabinet backs and cut-outs land in the right place.
  • Hardware: name the hinge and runner model, soft-close and load rating. "Soft-close hardware" alone is not a specification.
  • Electrical items: cabinet lighting and other electrical goods need UKCA marking, with certificates kept in your compliance pack.

Protecting your kitchen on the way to the UK

Agree early whether cabinets ship flat-packed or rigid. Flat-pack saves space, while rigid units reach site assembled but take up more room in the container. Doors, panels and worktops need corner protection, and every carton should be labelled by room and unit number so the fitter can find it. Ask for spare handles, hinges and filler panels to be included, because a replacement part from China can take weeks.

What a pre-shipment inspection should cover

Every order is inspected before shipping, and you receive a pre-shipment photo report. For kitchens, the checks should compare the finished goods with your approved schedule and sample:

  • Unit count and sizes against the cabinet schedule
  • Door colour and sheen against the approved sample
  • Door alignment, drawer action and hinge operation
  • Hardware models against the specification
  • Carton labels, packing and spare parts

What goes into the total cost

We price kitchens from your schedule, so we do not publish a price per kitchen. A headline figure means little until the board, doors, hardware and panels are fixed. Your quotation should show these as separate lines:

  • Factory goods, itemised by cabinet and panel
  • Our 5% sourcing fee on product value
  • Samples and inspection where they apply
  • Freight, customs clearance and UK delivery
  • Import duty and VAT, based on the goods being imported

Common questions

Is it worth buying a kitchen from China?

It can be when you have a measured layout, a settled specification and time for manufacture and shipping. It is usually not the right choice for an urgent kitchen or a single replacement cabinet.

How long does a kitchen from China take?

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site. Bespoke items typically take 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

Can you make a kitchen to my exact measurements?

Is there a minimum order?
