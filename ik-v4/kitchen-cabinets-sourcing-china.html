How to compare kitchen quotes properly

We compare carcass and back-board grade, door construction and finish, hardware models, drawer counts and every finishing panel. A room length is not a cabinet schedule: to quote accurately we need measured walls, ceiling heights, window and door openings, service positions and your appliance list.

Plywood, moisture-resistant MDF or MFC?

We can price comparable plywood, moisture-resistant MDF and particleboard (MFC) carcass options for the same layout. A cheaper quote often reflects a different board, door finish, hardware allowance or a missing panel schedule — so the choice is made against the actual specification, not the headline price.

Before manufacture

Final shop drawings, appliance ventilation and cut-outs are approved before production starts. Site survey, fitting, plumbing, electrics, worktops and appliances are excluded unless they're listed on your quotation.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.
