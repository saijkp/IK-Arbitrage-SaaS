How to ship from Alibaba to the UK

To ship an Alibaba order to the UK, agree the supplier terms, collect the packing and invoice details, then arrange freight, customs clearance and final delivery. You can use your own shipping agent for Alibaba orders, with LCL or FCL sea freight for the main shipment and air considered for urgent items.

Start with the supplier shipping terms

Ask for the three-letter term, named place or port and edition, for example Incoterms 2020. The term allocates transport costs, tasks and risk between seller and buyer; it is not a complete door-to-door quote.

  • EXW (Ex Works): the supplier makes the goods available at the agreed place. The buyer arranges collection, loading and export clearance as well as onward transport. Check who can complete the export formalities before agreeing.
  • FOB (Free On Board): the supplier clears the goods for export and puts them on board the vessel at the agreed origin port. The buyer arranges the main freight and onward import journey. FOB is a sea or inland-waterway term.
  • CIF (Cost, Insurance and Freight): the supplier pays freight and the required insurance to the named destination port, but risk passes when the goods are on board at origin. UK clearance, taxes and delivery need checking separately; CIF does not mean delivered to your door.
  • DDP (Delivered Duty Paid): the seller arranges transport and import clearance, including applicable duty and import VAT, to the named destination, ready for unloading. Confirm the importer and tax arrangements in writing.

Why use your own shipping agent for Alibaba?

A supplier-arranged shipment can be convenient. Buyers use an independent agent when they want to compare the full journey, coordinate several factories, or agree UK handling and delivery before booking. Compare both quotes on the same basis: collection point, packing, customs, taxes, delivery address and exclusions. Do not pay twice for freight already included in the supplier terms.

What to ask the supplier for

  • Commercial invoice: seller and buyer details, accurate goods descriptions, quantities, values, currency and agreed terms. A proforma can support an initial enquiry; obtain the final commercial invoice for shipment.
  • Packing list: contents of each carton or pallet, with marks that match the physical goods.
  • Carton details: carton count, external dimensions and gross weight, plus any pallets, crates or non-stackable pieces. Ask for packing photographs.
  • Ready date: when the packed order can actually be collected, including any inspection appointment.
  • Pickup address: full Chinese collection address, supplier contact and loading arrangements. Send the UK delivery postcode and access requirements too.

LCL, FCL or air for a typical Alibaba order?

LCL shares a container with other shipments and can suit a smaller order. FCL reserves a container for your shipment and can suit larger orders or goods needing less shared handling. Several suppliers can be consolidated for either approach. Compare total handling and delivery costs as well as freight; there is no universal size at which FCL wins.

Air can be considered for urgent items, samples or spare parts. Send the goods description, dimensions, weight and required date before choosing. Our LCL versus FCL comparison explains the practical trade-offs.

UK customs: importer, EORI, duty and import VAT

Agree who will be the importer before collection. For business imports into Great Britain, a GB EORI is needed. GOV.UK exempts goods that are both uncontrolled and for personal use only; Northern Ireland requirements can differ. Check your circumstances with the broker. See GOV.UK: importing goods into the UK and GOV.UK: who needs an EORI.

Goods must be declared and any duty and import VAT due accounted for. The commodity code affects duty; the goods value and customs valuation also matter. Do not treat the Alibaba purchase price as the complete tax calculation. See GOV.UK: paying VAT on imports and our furniture import duty and VAT guide.

Check the goods before they leave

Agree an optional pre-shipment inspection before release to transport. Give the inspector your specification, quantities, finish references and packing requirements. Resolve discrepancies with the supplier while the goods remain accessible. An inspection checks the agreed scope; it does not replace any product compliance documents or customs checks.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use my own shipping agent for Alibaba orders?

Yes. Agree the collection point and supplier shipping terms before booking. IK ENG can collect from any Chinese supplier, including goods you have already bought.

What documents should I request from my Alibaba supplier?

Request the commercial invoice, packing list, carton dimensions and weights, ready date and full pickup address with a contact. Share these with the shipping agent before collection.

Does CIF include UK delivery and taxes?

CIF covers the seller-arranged freight and required insurance to a named destination port. Check UK clearance, duty, import VAT and final delivery separately; CIF does not mean delivery to your door.

Can IK ENG check my goods before shipping?

Optional pre-shipment inspection can be arranged. Agree the inspection scope and timing before the goods leave the supplier.
