Hotel Furniture Supplier UK — Refit Without Blowing the Reopening Date

A China-sourced FF&E supplier for hotels and guest houses. Refits and refurbishments planned around your closure window, with every room matching and fire safety compliance confirmed before production, not after.

The problem

A hotel or guest house refit almost always happens in a tight window between seasons or during a planned closure — every extra day the rooms are out of service is trading days you don't get back. Guest-facing furniture also needs a finish standard above typical rental spec, every room needs to match, and compliance requirements are often more involved than domestic use, which isn't something you want to discover after the container has already shipped.

How we solve it

  • We review your brief, clarify missing information and agree the sourcing scope and programme before commitment.
  • One consolidated order so every room matches, whether it's a handful of rooms or a full property
  • We confirm the exact fire safety and compliance standard your project requires — domestic or commercial-grade — before production, with full documentation in your compliance pack
  • Pre-shipment inspection with a full photo report before the container seals, so you see exactly what's arriving before it leaves China
Tell us your required fire rating and compliance standard up front — we'll confirm what's achievable and get it in writing before anything goes into production.

What we need to quote a hotel refit

Send room types, occupancy, bed sizes, headboards, casegoods, seating, lighting and public-area quantities. We agree the intended contract use, finish samples and performance evidence before any product is selected.

Contract-grade versus domestic

Hospitality specifications can differ significantly from domestic furniture, and a domestic catalogue price doesn't prove a product is suitable for a hotel room. We source against contract-use requirements from the start.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.
