FF&E Sourcing for Hotels & Guest Houses — Refit Without Blowing the Reopening Date

Refits and refurbishments planned around your closure window, with every room matching and fire safety compliance confirmed before production, not after.

The problem

A hotel or guest house refit almost always happens in a tight window between seasons or during a planned closure — every extra day the rooms are out of service is trading days you don't get back. Guest-facing furniture also needs a finish standard above typical rental spec, every room needs to match, and compliance requirements are often more involved than domestic use, which isn't something you want to discover after the container has already shipped.

How we solve it

  • A confirmed delivery date planned around your closure or refit window, not a rough estimate
  • One consolidated order so every room matches, whether it's a handful of rooms or a full property
  • We confirm the exact fire safety and compliance standard your project requires — domestic or commercial-grade — before production, with full documentation in your compliance pack
  • Pre-shipment inspection with a full photo report before the container seals, so you see exactly what's arriving before it leaves China
Tell us your required fire rating and compliance standard up front — we'll confirm what's achievable and get it in writing before anything goes into production.
