How the comparisons work

CBM = length × width × height in metres × quantity. Sea LCL commonly compares 1 CBM with 1,000 kg: the larger of CBM and gross tonnes is shown as weight/measure units. Confirm the conversion, minimums and rounding with your forwarder.

Air volumetric weight commonly uses length × width × height in cm ÷ 6,000 per carton. Carriers vary: confirm the divisor and whether rounding and chargeable weight apply per package. The displayed air comparison uses shipment totals only.

For pallets or crates, use the complete packed envelope and avoid counting the cartons inside it again. Volume alone does not confirm container fit: door access, stacking and load limits also matter.
