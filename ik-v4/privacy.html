Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 September 2026 · IK Eng Ltd, 125 Great Clowes Street, Manchester M7 1AL

Who we are

IK Eng Ltd ("IK ENG", "we", "us") operates ikeng.co.uk, a China-sourcing service for UK property developers, architects and builders. This policy explains what data we collect when you use this website, why, and what rights you have over it.

What we collect

SourceWhat's collectedWhy
Site analytics (Google Analytics 4)Pages viewed, general location (country/city level), device/browser type, how you arrived at the site, and which links you click (e.g. WhatsApp, "send a brief")To understand which pages and content are actually helping visitors, and improve the site
"UK Buyer's Guide" download formName, email address, company (optional)To send you the guide by email, plus two follow-up emails over the following week (see "Follow-up emails" below)
"Project Sourcing Brief" formName, company, email, phone, your role, and the project details you choose to share (location, unit count, product categories, specification, budget range, timeline)To respond to your enquiry with sourcing options, pricing and a delivery estimate

We only collect what's listed above. We don't use tracking pixels from third-party advertisers, and we don't buy or sell contact lists.

Where it's stored

  • Form submissions are stored via Netlify Forms (our hosting provider) and in our own database (Firebase/Firestore), so we can respond to you and keep a record of the enquiry.
  • Guide-download and follow-up emails are sent via Resend, our email delivery provider, using only the name and email you provide. A short record (email, name, and whether you've unsubscribed) is kept via Netlify Blobs, our hosting provider's storage, purely to schedule and cancel these emails correctly.
  • Analytics data is processed by Google Analytics 4 under Google's own privacy terms.

Follow-up emails

If you download the UK Buyer's Guide, you'll get the guide immediately, then two further emails over the following week with practical sourcing information and a reminder to send us a brief if you have a live project. Every one of these emails includes an unsubscribe link — clicking it stops all future follow-ups (including any already scheduled) immediately, and we keep a record of that choice so it's respected even if you download the guide again later. We don't sell or share your email address for anyone else's marketing.

Cookies

This site uses cookies set by Google Analytics to recognise repeat visits and measure site usage. You'll see a cookie banner on your first visit — analytics cookies are only set if you accept them, in line with UK PECR (Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations). You can change your choice at any time by clearing your browser's cookies for this site and reloading the page.

How long we keep it

Enquiry and form data is kept for as long as reasonably needed to respond to you and maintain business records (typically up to 6 years, in line with standard UK record-keeping practice for commercial enquiries), after which it's deleted on request or on routine review.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to ask us what data we hold about you, correct it, or have it deleted. To do any of this, email sai@ikeng.co.uk — we'll respond within 30 days.

We never sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for marketing purposes. It's used only to run this business and respond to your enquiry.
