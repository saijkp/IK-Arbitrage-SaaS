What's in a package

Agree the contents room by room, including quantities, sizes, finishes and any exclusions.

  • Bedrooms: beds, headboards, bedside tables, wardrobes or hanging rails, drawers and mirrors
  • Living: sofas, chairs, coffee tables and media storage
  • Dining: tables, chairs and storage sized for the agreed guest capacity
  • Outdoor: tables, seating and storage suited to the location
  • Lighting and soft furnishings by agreement, with documentation and fitting scope recorded

Built for guest use

Specify durable finishes and cleaning instructions, washable or replaceable covers where suitable, and replaceable handles and fittings. Check sleeping layouts against usable room space, circulation and luggage storage. Upholstery and mattresses for lets need fire-safety evidence appropriate to the product and use: agree the specification with your adviser and read our hotel and holiday let furniture fire safety guide. We check documentation is supplied; we do not certify products.

Multiple lets or a park

Use a repeat specification across properties, with a separate schedule for each layout. Agree phased deliveries and property-labelled packing so a later opening does not fill the first property with stored furniture. Keep finish references and spare-part details for replacements, subject to availability.

Outdoor extras

Add outdoor furniture, a sauna or cold plunge or an aluminium pergola to the brief. Confirm base, planning, drainage, electrical supply and installation arrangements with the relevant professionals before approving dimensions.

How it works

Send plans and a room list. We return factory options within 48 hours of a usable brief, then agree drawings, finish samples and the final schedule before production. A pre-shipment inspection checks the goods against that schedule before packing and delivery to your UK address. Offloading and installation are agreed separately.

What we need to quote

Send the property count, floor plans or room photos, sleeping capacity, bed sizes, finish references, delivery postcode and access, opening dates and budget. List items you are keeping and outdoor extras separately.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 3–5% of product value, depending on order size, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.

Payment: 50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading. Shipping, duty and VAT are invoiced separately.

Frequently asked questions

Can you furnish a whole holiday let?

Yes. Agree a room-by-room schedule for bedrooms, living, dining and outdoor areas, with lighting and soft furnishings included by agreement.

Can several properties use the same package?

Yes. Repeat the approved specification and record differences in room dimensions, sleeping layouts and quantities for each property.

Can a sauna or pergola ship alongside the furniture?

We can include these in the sourcing brief and assess combined shipping. Dimensions, packing and site requirements need checking before the order is agreed.

Do you check furniture fire-safety paperwork?

We check the requested documentation is supplied; we do not certify products. Ask Trading Standards or a fire safety professional to review the requirements for your property.

What are the payment terms?

50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading. Shipping, duty and VAT are invoiced separately.
