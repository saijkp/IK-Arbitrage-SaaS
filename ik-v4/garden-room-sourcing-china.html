What we can source

Compare timber-frame, steel-frame and prefabricated panel structures, supplied as kits or assembled modules where transport and access allow. Options include timber or composite cladding, glazing layouts, doors and roof finishes. Factory availability and suitability are checked for each brief.

Insulation and a combined garden room + sauna

Request wall, roof and floor insulation options with material types, thicknesses and factory-stated U-values. Your builder should review thermal bridges, vapour control, ventilation and condensation risk for the UK site. We check that factory documentation is supplied; we do not certify performance or promise year-round use. A combined garden room and sauna needs a coordinated layout, moisture separation and equipment review. See our sauna sourcing service.

How it works

Send your brief and drawings → receive factory options in 48 hours → approve the specification, quotation and drawings → production → pre-shipment inspection against the agreed specification → delivery to your UK address. Confirm any corrections before loading and agree the delivery scope in writing.

What your builder does

Involve your own builder before approving drawings. They assess the ground, design and prepare the base, plan anchoring, assemble the kit or position the module, and complete weatherproofing and site finishes. A qualified electrician assesses the supply and handles electrical installation, testing and any applicable notification. IK ENG does not install. Ask your builder which structural calculations and product documents they need before ordering.

Planning and Building Regulations

For England, read GOV.UK permitted development guidance and our outdoor sauna planning guide. Some domestic outbuildings may fall within permitted development, subject to conditions on use, height, position and land coverage. Check with your council; do not assume domestic rights cover holiday lets or hospitality sites. Rules elsewhere in the UK differ.

Building Regulations are a separate assessment. Small detached buildings without sleeping accommodation may qualify for exemptions, depending on floor area, boundary distance and construction. Sleeping use or a floor area above 30 square metres needs particular attention from building control. Check the exact proposal with your council or a qualified professional before ordering; we do not promise compliance.

Delivery and access

Agree packed dimensions, weights, crate or module sizes and lifting points before choosing a shipping method. Check road access, gates, overhead cables, turning space and the route to the base with your builder and delivery contractor. A crane or other lifting equipment may be needed. Offloading, lifting, permits and movement into the garden must be agreed separately; delivery to an address does not include installation.

What we need to quote

Send the external size and height, intended use, drawings or reference pictures, UK postcode, access measurements and photographs, preferred structure and finish, glazing and insulation requirements, timeframe and budget. Tell us whether you have a builder and whether you want a sauna included.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 3–5% of product value, depending on order size, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.

Payment: 50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading. Shipping, duty and VAT are invoiced separately.

Frequently asked questions

Does IK ENG install garden rooms?

No. IK ENG sources, inspects before shipping and arranges delivery to your UK address. Your own builder handles the base, assembly and site work, with electrics by a qualified electrician.

Can you source a garden room with a sauna?

Yes. We can request factory options for a combined garden room and sauna, with separate specifications for the room, sauna, ventilation and electrical equipment for your builder to review.

Will an insulated garden room suit my intended use?

Ask your builder to review the complete wall, roof, floor, glazing and ventilation specification for the site and intended use. We check that factory documentation is supplied; we do not certify insulation performance or promise year-round use.

Do I need planning permission?

That depends on the site, dimensions and use. Check with your council before ordering, particularly for sleeping accommodation, holiday lets or hospitality sites. Planning and Building Regulations are separate checks.

What are the payment terms and lead times?

Payment is 50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading. Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the order milestones before you commit.
