HMO kitchen cost: plan the shared kitchen

An HMO kitchen cost starts with the residents, layout and required provision. No per-kitchen price is given here. Use this worksheet to compare the same scope, from cabinet construction to UK fitting and connection work.

Start with residents and council amenity standards

Record the intended number of residents and who shares each kitchen. Council amenity standards address kitchen provision in relation to occupancy: cooking, washing-up, food preparation, storage and refrigeration. Check the current standards and licence conditions with your own council before fixing the layout or ordering cabinets.

For an example of published council guidance, see Manchester City Council’s HMO standards. Do not apply another council’s provision schedule to your property without checking. Show the proposed appliances, sinks, preparation space and storage on measured plans for review.

Specify HMO kitchen cabinets from China

Compare carcasses by substrate, thickness, moisture protection, edging and back-panel construction. Set door material and finish separately: laminate-faced board, painted doors and veneer involve different cleaning and repair considerations. Ask for a sample of the actual construction rather than approving a colour photograph.

Contract-grade construction can be specified with 18 mm boards and commercial hardware, checked at inspection. Record the agreed board specification, hinge and runner references, exposed edges and replaceable parts. Provide a lockable food cupboard per resident in the purchasing schedule, with clear allocation and a key plan; confirm the storage arrangement against your council’s requirements. This is a planning brief, not a claim that identical lock requirements apply everywhere.

No per-kitchen figure is given because a measured specification is needed. For published indicative furniture figures, read HMO furnishing cost planning and hotel bedroom furniture costs. Those figures describe their own furniture scope; they are not kitchen allowances.

What to source in China and what to arrange in the UK

Cabinet carcasses, doors, panels, handles and agreed accessories can be sourced from China against approved drawings. Sinks and worktop products can be considered where compatibility, packing and site finishing are resolved. Compare appliances with UK supply on documented compliance, service support and delivery coordination rather than assuming they belong in the import order.

Keep site templating, plumbing, electrical connections, fire-door work and extraction design and installation with suitable UK trades. Agree service positions and appliance dimensions before cabinet manufacture. A cabinet order does not approve the property’s fire precautions or ventilation. Read UK appliance size checks alongside the drawings.

Flat-pack shipping, consolidation and inspection

Specify flat-pack shipping where the selected cabinet system supports it. Ask for actual packed dimensions and an assembly plan; do not assume a freight saving without comparing the packed quotation. We consolidate at our warehouse in China. Label cartons by property, kitchen and cabinet reference, with matching hardware packs and a master packing list.

Inspection should check dimensions, board and door specification, hardware, locks, finish, quantities and packing against approved samples and drawings. Compliance is confirmed per order from factory test evidence, including UKCA on electrical goods. Resolve discrepancies before loading. The kitchen cabinet middleman recovery case study is relevant background when planning specification checks.

Keep the delivered budget itemised

List product value, the IK ENG sourcing fee of 5% on product value, samples, inspection, freight, insurance, customs clearance, duty, import VAT, UK delivery and installation separately. Ask which charges are included, estimated or excluded, and who pays each supplier. Payment is 50% deposit / 50% after pre-shipment inspection before loading. Confirm logistics payment dates in the written quotation.

There is no minimum to enquire; factory MOQs vary by product. Sample/model-room costs are confirmed in writing before you pay. Our own team can carry in, assemble and install as a separately quoted line, or you can use your own fitters. Confirm the handover between furniture assembly and specialist UK trades.

Frequently asked questions

How much does an HMO kitchen cost?

No per-kitchen figure is given here. Price the measured cabinet schedule, worktops, sinks, appliances, resident storage, logistics and UK installation separately.

How many kitchen facilities does my HMO need?

Check your council’s current amenity standards and the property’s licence conditions against the intended residents and shared layout before ordering.

Can I source HMO kitchen cabinets from China?

Yes, against approved measurements, construction details and drawings. Confirm appliance fit, flat-pack assembly, inspection and UK installation responsibilities before manufacture.

Should each resident have a lockable food cupboard?

Include a lockable food cupboard per resident in the purchasing brief and confirm the storage arrangement with your council. Keep shared utensil storage separate.

Who installs the kitchen?

Our own team can carry in, assemble and install as a separately quoted line, or you can use your own fitters. Leave plumbing, electrical connections, fire-door work and extraction to suitable UK trades.
