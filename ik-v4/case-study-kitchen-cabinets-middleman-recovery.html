Kitchen Cabinet Container Bought Through a Middleman: What Went Wrong and How It Was Recovered

A customer ordered a container of kitchen cabinets through a company they believed was the factory. It was a trading company presenting itself as the manufacturer.

IK ENG inspected the cabinets and got the factory to rework them, saving the customer money. IK ENG expedited the whole process, and the order is on track for delivery before the customer's target date.

Order
A container of kitchen cabinets
Amount paid
USD 140,000
Factory pricing
The same order could have cost around a quarter of that, based on factory pricing.

What went wrong

Instructions passed through the intermediary to the actual manufacturer. This led to miscommunication, and production took a long time.

The cabinets were not produced to the agreed specifications. Cheap, lower-quality materials were used, and the board sheets were not properly hot-pressed. There were many other issues with the order.

What IK ENG did

The customer then came to IK ENG.

  1. IK ENG inspected the cabinets.
  2. IK ENG got the factory to rework them, saving the customer money.
  3. IK ENG expedited the whole process. The order is on track for delivery before the customer's target date.

Photos from our inspection of this order at the factory.

How to avoid this

The following is general buyer guidance, separate from the account above.

  • Verify that you are dealing with the manufacturer. Arrange a factory visit or live video walkthrough and check the business licence scope against the manufacturing activity. Our sourcing, inspection and factory visits service can help with supplier checks.
  • Agree written specifications and drawings before production, including board type and thickness, finish and hardware. Use our kitchen cabinet checks before ordering to prepare them.
  • Approve samples against the written specification. See our guide to ordering samples for how to record approvals.
  • Arrange pre-shipment inspection before the balance is paid. The furniture pre-shipment inspection checklist covers checking goods against approved references.
  • Agree payment terms in writing. IK ENG's terms are 50% deposit / 50% after pre-shipment inspection before loading.
