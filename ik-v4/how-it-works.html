How it works

How China Sourcing with IK ENG Works — Process, Fees and Timing

Everything you need to know before sending a brief: the steps, what we charge, how long it takes, what your quotation shows, and rough budgets per room.

The process, step by step

  1. Send your brief. Floor plans, a room list, inspiration images, delivery postcode, approximate budget and required arrival date. A mood board or photo is enough to start.
  2. Factory options within 48 hours. We clarify the scope, identify suitable suppliers and compare their offers — we aim for several comparable factory prices before recommending one.
  3. Itemised quotation. Products, finishes, drawings, costs and exclusions are set out for your approval before anything is ordered.
  4. Samples and approval. Where needed, samples or drawings are approved before bulk production starts.
  5. Production and inspection. Goods are inspected before shipment against the approved specification, with a photo report.
  6. Shipping, customs and delivery. Freight, UK customs clearance, duty and final delivery to your site are coordinated for you.

Our fee

We charge a 5% sourcing fee on product value. It covers supplier search and comparison, factory liaison and quality-control coordination, and it's shown as its own line on every quotation — never hidden in the product price. Freight, duty and delivery are shown separately at cost. Inspections, buying visits and other specialist services are priced to the agreed scope.

What your quotation shows

  • An itemised product schedule with dimensions, quantities, materials, finishes and hardware
  • Supplier lead times and packing
  • Transport, customs clearance and import duty
  • Samples, design work, inspection, installation and specialist delivery as separate lines where they apply

Final prices depend on the approved specification and delivery arrangements.

Payment terms

A 50% deposit confirms your order and starts production. The balance is due on delivery. Payment milestones are written into every quotation.

Minimum orders

Single-home and smaller enquiries are welcome, and there's no container minimum to make an enquiry. Factory minimum quantities and the practical cost of shipping are checked for each product — a single item can be less economical than a consolidated room or house order, and we'll tell you if UK stock would suit you better.

How long it takes

  • Brief and specification: allow 1–2 weeks, longer if drawings or details are still being finalised
  • Standard products: typically 10–12 weeks to site — roughly 3–5 weeks production, 5–6 weeks sea freight, then customs and delivery
  • Bespoke kitchens, wardrobes and joinery: plan on 12–18 weeks from approved drawings and samples
  • Urgent orders: air freight is available at higher cost

Supplier holidays — especially Chinese New Year — and sample revisions can add time. Your order gets its own programme and a confirmed delivery date once the supplier and shipping are booked.

Quality and compliance

Upholstered furniture is supplied to UK furniture fire safety requirements (BS 7177 and the Furniture and Furnishings Fire Safety Regulations), and electrical items carry UKCA marking. We check certificates against the exact model, finish and construction you're ordering — a catalogue certificate for a different model isn't accepted — and agree the inspection checks before shipment.
