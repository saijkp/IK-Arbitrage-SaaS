Specification that matters

Specify the aluminium alloy and wall thickness of posts and beams; powder-coat finish, including the coating brand, thickness and RAL colour; blade size and seal/gutter design so water drains through the posts; motor brand and control system; and stainless fixings. The factory confirms these in writing per quote.

Wind, snow and the UK weather

Pergolas must be specified for the site's wind exposure and snow load. Ask the factory for its design wind and snow load figures and check them against your location. Coastal and exposed sites need heavier sections and corrosion-resistant finishes. Footings and the base must suit the post loads.

Planning, building regs and electrics

Many garden pergolas fall under permitted development, but height, size and position limits apply. Listed buildings, flats and front gardens differ: check with your local council, including whether building regulations apply to your proposal. Commercial installations at pubs, restaurants and hotels may need planning permission.

Motorised roofs, lights and heaters need wiring by a qualified electrician to BS 7671. We check UKCA or CE documentation, wiring diagrams and English manuals are supplied. Installation is not included unless quoted.

Shipping and delivery

Pergolas ship as profiles and components in crates. Long post and beam lengths affect crate size and freight cost. Single units usually go LCL; multiple units or installer stock can fill a container.

We confirm the HS code and duty before you commit. Aluminium products can be subject to trade-remedy measures; we check whether any apply to the exact product at quotation.

Inspection before shipping

We check profile dimensions and wall thickness, powder-coat finish and colour against the approved sample, blade operation and a motor test-run, fixings and parts count against the packing list, and crate packing for long profiles. See our China sourcing inspection and visits service.

What we need to quote

Send width × projection × height, wall-mounted or freestanding, roof type, side screens wanted, colour (RAL), motor, lighting and heater options, site postcode and exposure (coastal or exposed), quantity, budget and timing. Include photos of the space or reference links; Alibaba and 1688 links are welcome.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Questions about pergolas from China

Are Chinese aluminium pergolas good quality?

Quality varies by factory. Specify the aluminium alloy, wall thickness, coating and motor, and inspect before shipping rather than relying on blanket quality claims.

Can you make a pergola to my exact size?

Yes. Pergolas can be made to measure from your dimensions and drawings. Confirm the drawings before production.

Do I need planning permission?

Often not for a domestic garden, but height, size and position limits apply. Check with your local council; commercial sites often need planning permission.

Can I buy just one?

Yes. A single unit usually ships LCL. Shipping cost per unit is lower when ordering several.

Can I install it myself?

Fixed-roof kits can suit a competent installer. Motorised and electrical parts need a qualified electrician, and footings must suit the loads.
