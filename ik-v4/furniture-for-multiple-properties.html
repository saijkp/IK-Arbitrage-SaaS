Furniture for Property Investors and Multiple Properties

Furniture for investment properties works best with one clear specification. Our landlord furniture packages bring room schedules, repeat products and delivery stages together across your portfolio.

The problem

Buying furniture for one property at a time from UK retail is slow and expensive on its own — do it across five, ten, or fifty properties and it compounds: separate orders, separate delivery dates, inconsistent stock between batches bought months apart, and retail markup paid every single time instead of once at scale.

How we solve it

  • One consolidated order regardless of how many units — the process doesn't get more complicated as the number grows, the paperwork does, and we handle that
  • One factory-confirmed spec applied identically across every property, so unit 1 and unit 40 match exactly
  • Freight consolidated across the whole order, which genuinely brings the per-unit landed cost down compared to buying the same items one property at a time
  • Staggered delivery if properties come online at different times — you're not forced to take everything on one date if that doesn't suit your schedule
  • Every order inspected before shipping, with a full pre-shipment photo report before the container seals.
Growing a portfolio? Send us what you need for the properties you have now — we can quote the next batch on the same terms when you're ready, rather than starting from scratch each time.

One matrix for every property

We use a property-by-property matrix listing room types, quantities, addresses, access restrictions and delivery dates. Standard items are kept separate from exceptions, so repeated products stay comparable across the portfolio.

Where consolidation helps

Consolidating orders can improve shipping economics, but the saving depends on volume, route, packing and storage. We show you the figures on your quotation rather than promising a fixed discount.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.

Landlord furniture package FAQs

Can one landlord furniture package cover different properties?

Yes. We use a master specification and a separate schedule for each address, with room dimensions, quantities and exceptions recorded before ordering.

Can you deliver furniture in stages?

Yes. We plan deliveries around each property's readiness, with a receiving contact, access details and storage arrangements agreed for each stage.

How much does furniture sourcing cost?

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. There is no minimum to enquire. Pay a 50% deposit to place the order, 30% after you approve a video of your finished goods at the factory, and 20% on delivery.

Can we reorder furniture for later investment properties?

Keep the approved product references, finishes and room schedules. We check availability and factory quantities for the next order. Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items take 12–18 weeks from approved drawings.
