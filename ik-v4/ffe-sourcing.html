What FF&E sourcing means with IK ENG

FF&E is everything that furnishes a building once the construction work is done. We act as your China sourcing agent for that package: we turn your drawings or room schedule into a costed FF&E schedule, compare factory offers, agree samples and specifications, arrange inspection before shipping and coordinate delivery to your UK site.

How FF&E procurement works

  1. Brief. Send drawings, a room schedule or reference images, quantities, delivery postcode, budget and timing.
  2. Costed FF&E schedule. Room by room: product, dimensions, materials, finish, quantity and GBP price, with furniture, kitchens, bathrooms, lighting and installation kept as separate lines.
  3. Samples and approvals. Finish samples, drawings and, for repeated rooms, a sample-room or mock-up approval before production.
  4. Production and inspection. Every order inspected before shipping, with a pre-shipment photo report before the container seals.
  5. Shipping and delivery. Packing, shipping, import and delivery arrangements and exclusions are set out in your quotation, with phased delivery by block or handover stage where needed.

UK compliance built into the schedule

Compliance is confirmed before production, not after the container arrives. Your compliance pack includes BS 7177 evidence for mattresses and beds, permanent fire labels on relevant upholstered items under the Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations, and UKCA or CE conformity documents for electrical goods. For hotels and other contract use, we agree the required standard in writing before any product is selected. See our furniture fire safety guide.

FF&E sourcing: frequently asked questions

What does FF&E sourcing include?

FF&E means furniture, fixtures and equipment. We source loose furniture, beds and mattresses, fitted wardrobes and joinery, kitchens, bathroom fittings, lighting and selected soft furnishings, each as its own line on a costed schedule.

Do you only work on large projects?

No. There is no minimum to enquire. We work on single properties as well as multi-unit schemes, and check supplier minimums and shipping practicality for each product.

How is FF&E procurement priced?

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply. Pay a 50% deposit to place the order, 30% after factory video confirmation of your finished goods, and 20% on delivery.

How long does FF&E from China take?

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items take 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We return factory options and pricing within 48 hours of your brief.

Is the FF&E compliant with UK fire safety rules?

We confirm compliance before production: BS 7177 evidence for mattresses and beds, permanent fire labels on relevant upholstered items under the Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations, and UKCA marking on electrical goods. Documents go in your compliance pack.

Can you prepare the FF&E specification if we have no designer?

Yes. Our in-house designer can prepare room layouts, a mood board and a product schedule for a fixed fee agreed before we start, credited against your order if you go ahead. See our design and specification service.

Got an FF&E package to price?

Send your plans, schedule or reference images, delivery postcode, approximate budget and required timing. We'll come back within 48 hours with factory options and pricing.
