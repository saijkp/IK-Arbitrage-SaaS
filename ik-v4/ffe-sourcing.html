What FF&E sourcing means with IK ENG

FF&E is everything that furnishes a building once the construction work is done. We act as your China sourcing agent for that package: we turn your drawings or room schedule into a costed FF&E schedule, compare factory offers, agree samples and specifications, arrange inspection before shipping and coordinate delivery to your UK site.

You approve every product, drawing, cost and order term before commitment. Factory options and pricing come back within 48 hours of your brief.

Hospitality and project procurement

Plan guest bedrooms, reception areas, lounges and shared spaces as separate parts of the FF&E schedule. Record intended use, quantities, finish references and approval responsibilities before comparing suppliers. Coordinate procurement with the fit-out programme and site access requirements.

How FF&E procurement works

  1. Brief. Send drawings, a room schedule or reference images, quantities, delivery postcode, budget and timing.
  2. Costed FF&E schedule. Room by room: product, dimensions, materials, finish, quantity and £ price, with furniture, kitchens, bathrooms, lighting and installation kept as separate lines.
  3. Samples and approvals. Finish samples, drawings and, for repeated rooms, a sample-room or mock-up approval before production.
  4. Production and inspection. Every order inspected before shipping, with a pre-shipment photo report before the container seals.
  5. Shipping and delivery. Packing, shipping, import and delivery arrangements and exclusions are set out in your quotation, with phased delivery by block or handover stage where needed.

UK compliance built into the schedule

Compliance is confirmed before production, not after delivery. For each order we get the factory's test evidence: BS 7177 for beds and mattresses, permanent fire labels on upholstered items (BS 5852 / BS EN 1021, Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations), and UKCA marking on electrical goods. For contract use we specify contract-grade construction (18 mm boards, commercial hardware) and check it at our pre-shipment inspection.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Sample price, making time and courier cost are confirmed in writing before you pay. Where the factory offers it, sample cost is deducted from the order. For repeated rooms we recommend one model room approved before full production, quoted as its own line.

Our sourcing fee is 3–5% of product value, depending on order size, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply. Pay a 50% deposit to place the order, the 50% balance after our pre-shipment inspection (photos and video of your finished, packed goods), before loading; shipping, duty and VAT are invoiced separately.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. See China-to-UK production and shipping times.

No minimum to enquire. MOQs vary by factory, product and finish. On a recent 56-room order, factories accepted 56 sets of each item. We can combine goods from several factories into one shipment at our warehouse in China, so small quantities still ship economically.

FF&E means furniture, fixtures and equipment. We source loose furniture, beds and mattresses, fitted wardrobes and joinery, kitchens, bathroom fittings, lighting and selected soft furnishings, each as its own line on a costed schedule.
