What we make

  • Fitted wardrobes and sliding-door wardrobes
  • Dressing rooms and walk-in storage
  • Utility and study storage
  • Media units
  • Bathroom joinery
  • Wall panelling and display units

What we need to design and quote

Overall and internal dimensions, hanging lengths, shelving, drawers, door type, opening clearances, hardware and any integrated lighting. If you have a particular style in mind — for example an Indian-style wardrobe with a specific compartment layout — reference images and a list of the internal compartments get the arrangement right far better than a style label alone.

Measurements before final price

Room measurements and an approved unit schedule come before final pricing. Sloping ceilings, corners, skirting, sockets and uneven walls all need allowing for, and finish samples and edge details are agreed before production. Joinery is priced from your unit schedule rather than a flat rate per metre, so you pay for what you actually get.

What's separate

Installation, site templating, wall preparation, electrical work and design fees are agreed separately and shown as their own lines on your quotation.

Fees, timing & minimum orders
  • 5% sourcing fee on product value, shown on every quotation
  • Standard items typically 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings
  • No minimum to enquire — supplier minimums are checked product by product
