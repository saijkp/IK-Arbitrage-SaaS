MOQ for Furniture from China: Can You Order One Piece?

MOQ means minimum order quantity, and furniture MOQs vary widely by factory, product and finish. You can enquire about one piece with IK ENG: there is no minimum to enquire, but supplier acceptance and the practicality of UK shipping must be checked.

What does MOQ mean for furniture?

MOQ means minimum order quantity: the smallest order a supplier will accept on the terms offered. It may apply to an entire order, a particular design, a material, or each colour and finish. Ask which unit the minimum applies to before comparing quotes. MOQs vary widely by factory and product.

Why factories set minimum orders

Production takes preparation: machinery setup, cutting plans, material purchasing and packing arrangements. A factory may need enough orders to justify a production run or to use a purchased batch of fabric, board, veneer or hardware. A bespoke colour or finish can introduce another batch requirement even when the furniture design is standard.

Ask whether the minimum comes from the factory's production process or a component supplier. That answer helps you decide whether changing the finish, selecting another material or choosing a standard design would make the order workable.

Stock and standard ranges versus bespoke furniture

An item already in stock may be available in a smaller quantity than a fresh production run, subject to the supplier's selling terms. A standard range can use established components and finishes. Bespoke dimensions, upholstery or finishes need a separate check because the factory may have to buy materials and prepare production specifically for your order.

Ask for availability, the minimum per finish and whether a repeat order would need a fresh batch. Keep approved samples and specifications: a later finish batch may not match an earlier one exactly.

Can I order one piece of furniture from China?

You can enquire about one piece. Whether it is practical depends on the supplier's minimum, availability and the total cost of packing, handling, freight and UK delivery. There is no promise that every factory will accept a single-piece order. Compare the complete quotation with a locally available option before committing.

Ways to work with factory MOQs

  • Mix items within an order: ask whether several standard designs can meet an order minimum together. A minimum per colour or model may still apply.
  • Consolidate suppliers: IK ENG can combine goods from more than one supplier into one shipment. This brings separate purchases together for transport; each factory's minimum still needs to be agreed.
  • Choose standard finishes: ask what the factory already produces, rather than specifying a new colour or material batch.
  • Compare LCL and full-container shipping: less-than-container-load (LCL) shares container space with other shipments; a full container is booked for your shipment. Ask for both suitable options, including handling, packing and delivery charges. A factory MOQ is separate from a freight booking.
  • Coordinate the wider project: combine a realistic room schedule before requesting quotes. Avoid ordering unwanted items just to reach a minimum.
