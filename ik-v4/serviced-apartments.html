FF&E Sourcing for Serviced Apartments — Consistent Spec Across Every Unit

Guests compare units directly, on the listing photos and in person. One factory-confirmed spec sourced from China, applied identically across every apartment, delivered together in time for launch.

The problem

A serviced apartment block only works commercially if every unit performs the same — guests booking "the same" listing expect the same finish, and a mismatched sofa or a cheaper substitute in unit 12 shows up in reviews. At the same time, the furniture takes more wear than a standard residential let: more turnover, more cleaning, more use per year. And because opening or relaunch dates are usually fixed and publicly booked against, a late delivery isn't just inconvenient — it's lost revenue on rooms that can't go live.

How we solve it

  • One confirmed spec sourced and produced for the whole block, so unit 4 matches unit 40 exactly — not "close enough"
  • Rental-durable material and hardware as the default assumption, specified for turnover, not showroom presentation
  • Pre-shipment inspection on the full order — not a sample — with a photo report before the container seals
  • Consolidated delivery so units go live together, timed against your opening or relaunch date
  • No minimum order — works whether it's a handful of units or a full block

What "furnishing package" means here

One spec sheet, agreed and factory-confirmed before production, applied to every unit in the block — not a rough brief interpreted differently per apartment. Product names, quantities, finish, and a single delivery date for the whole package.

Multi-block or phased opening? We can stagger delivery to match your rollout, the same way we do for multi-block residential schemes.

Planning your apartment mix

Agree the apartment mix, guest capacity, sofa-bed requirements, storage and loose accessories up front. We then build one repeatable specification with approved sample finishes and a replacement plan, so unit 30 matches unit 1.

What a package includes

Furniture packages don't automatically include kitchenware, linen, appliances, TVs, broadband or installation. We can add any of these once there's an inventory with quantities, and they're listed as separate lines on your quotation.
