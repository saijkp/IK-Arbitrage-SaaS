How it works for serviced apartment operators

  1. Define the apartment mix. Send plans and quantities for each layout, sleeping capacity, sofa-bed requirements, budget, postcode and opening programme.
  2. Check guest and housekeeping use. Review sofa beds open and closed, dining capacity, luggage storage, cleaning access and care instructions. Confirm finishes and product evidence before repeating a selection.
  3. Approve a repeatable specification. Record each product, quantity, dimension and finish, with samples and fitted-item drawings where needed. Name the person who approves substitutions.
  4. Inspect and label by unit. Compare production with the approved schedule and receive a pre-shipment photo report. Agree unit labels and the packing list for delivery checks.
  5. Plan the launch sequence. Agree a delivery window or phased opening schedule, receiving contact and access arrangements. Allow for assembly, snagging, cleaning and listing photographs before guests arrive.

A furniture inventory and an operating inventory

A sofa bed changes both sleeping capacity and usable living space. Check the open footprint against tables and circulation routes, then review seating and dining places for the advertised guest capacity. Keep kitchenware, linen, lamps and accessories identifiable on the inventory. Record care instructions and replaceable components before approving finishes for repeated cleaning.

Serviced apartment operators FAQs

How do you plan different serviced apartment layouts?

Use a specification for each apartment type, with separate dimensions and quantities. Match sleeping, seating and dining capacity to the guest numbers, and record differences rather than copying one layout across every unit.

Does a serviced apartment furniture pack include linen and kitchenware?

Not automatically. Kitchenware, linen, appliances, TVs, broadband and installation need an agreed inventory and separate quotation lines.

What should I check before adding sofa beds?

Record the sofa bed dimensions open and closed, circulation space and guest capacity. Review the mechanism, care instructions and cleaning access before approving the selection.

Can deliveries follow a phased apartment opening?

Yes. Delivery stages can be planned around the rollout. Agree the unit allocation, receiving contact, access and storage arrangements for each stage, with time for assembly and cleaning before guests arrive.
