What is DDP shipping? DDP vs DAP for UK buyers

DDP means Delivered Duty Paid: under Incoterms 2020 the seller handles import clearance and applicable import duties and taxes as well as transport to the named destination. DAP means Delivered at Place: the seller transports the goods there, but the buyer handles import clearance and applicable duty and import VAT. Both deliver ready for unloading from the arriving vehicle.

DDP and DAP under Incoterms 2020

The key difference is responsibility for import clearance and its costs. Under DDP the seller takes that responsibility; under DAP it sits with the buyer. For both, the seller bears transport risk to the named destination, where delivery takes place with the goods available on the arriving vehicle, ready to unload. The buyer normally handles unloading. See ICC Academy: DAP and DDP under Incoterms 2020.

Write the exact destination and Incoterms 2020 in the agreement. A port, depot and your site are different delivery points. Confirm unloading arrangements separately. Incoterms allocate contractual tasks, costs and risk; they do not replace customs law.

Who pays UK duty and import VAT?

Under an unmodified DDP contract, the seller bears applicable UK duty and import VAT as part of import clearance. Under DAP, the buyer bears those import charges and clearance. A quote labelled DDP but excluding import VAT needs clarification and an explicit written agreement about the changed allocation. See ICC Academy: DAP and DDP under Incoterms 2020.

UK goods must still be declared and any duty and VAT due accounted for: GOV.UK: paying VAT on imports. The delivery label does not remove tax obligations. Identify the importer, customs representative and tax payment arrangement before dispatch. See our furniture duty and VAT guide.

Pros and cons for UK buyers

  • DDP: a clear delivered offer can simplify budgeting and reduce the buyer’s coordination tasks. It depends on a workable import arrangement and transparent tax documentation. Ask who is named on the declaration.
  • DAP: the buyer can organise import clearance with its own broker and see the customs paperwork directly. The buyer must plan the separate tax and clearance charges and provide the necessary information.
  • Either option: confirm the delivery point, unloading, exclusions and how unexpected storage or examination charges will be handled before accepting.

Do not assume that paying a DDP invoice lets your business reclaim import VAT. HMRC sets conditions for recovery and evidence; ask your broker or accountant to check your position. See GOV.UK: paying VAT on imports.

Why a DDP price should still be itemised

Ask for collection, export clearance, main freight, destination handling, brokerage, duty, import VAT, final delivery and any insurance to be shown separately. A single total makes it hard to compare scope or see whether taxes have been included twice. Ask which lines are estimates, what assumptions they use and which documents will confirm the final amounts.

The quote should identify the goods and declared values accurately. Keep the supplier invoice, packing list, agreed terms and clearance records. Our shipping costs guide walks through each line.

Frequently asked questions

What does DDP mean in shipping?

DDP means Delivered Duty Paid. Under Incoterms 2020 the seller arranges transport and import clearance, bearing applicable import duty and taxes, to the named destination ready for unloading.

What is the difference between DDP and DAP?

Under DDP the seller handles import clearance and applicable import duty and taxes. Under DAP the buyer handles those responsibilities. Both deliver to the named destination ready for unloading.

Does DDP include UK import VAT?

Under an unmodified DDP contract, applicable import VAT is for the seller’s account. Confirm the importer, tax arrangement and any proposed exclusions in writing before shipping.
