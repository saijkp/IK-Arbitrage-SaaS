This is a separate shipping service from IK ENG Ltd. You can buy from any Chinese supplier, whether you have already ordered or are planning a purchase. There is no minimum to enquire. Each job is quoted individually, with every cost on its own line.

Who it is for

  • Buyers ordering through Alibaba, Made-in-China or 1688, or directly from their own factory.
  • Small businesses needing goods collected in China and delivered in the UK.
  • Landlords and developers who have bought direct for a property or site.
  • E-commerce sellers arranging sea freight for stock.

What we handle

  1. Agree your goods, supplier details, ready date and UK delivery requirements.
  2. Arrange collection from your supplier in China.
  3. Consolidate goods from more than one supplier into one shipment where needed.
  4. Arrange export paperwork and the agreed LCL/FCL sea freight or air freight.
  5. Arrange UK customs clearance through an authorised UK customs broker, using the import option agreed in your quotation.
  6. Arrange delivery to your UK address or site.

LCL or FCL sea freight?

LCL: shared container / part load

Your goods share a container with other cargo. This can make sense when your shipment does not need a full container.

FCL: full container

A full container can make sense for a larger shipment. We assess your goods, packing and delivery requirements before quoting.

Air freight for urgent shipments

Alongside our main LCL/FCL sea freight service, we arrange air freight from China to the UK for urgent consignments, samples for approval before a production order, spare parts and replacement components, and chemicals.

Chemicals can be sent by air with a Safety Data Sheet (SDS). Hazardous chemicals are accepted only where airline dangerous-goods rules allow, and must be agreed in advance.

Air freight costs more than sea freight and is quoted per shipment. Send the goods details and required date so we can confirm suitability before booking.

You as importer, or DDP on request

You are the importer: duty and import VAT are shown separately on your quotation. UK customs clearance is arranged through an authorised UK customs broker.

DDP (delivered duty paid): on request, we can quote one delivered price including UK duty and import VAT, with IK ENG handling them. The quotation confirms the agreed import arrangement and itemises every cost.

What we need from you to quote

  • Supplier name and city in China.
  • What the goods are.
  • Number of cartons and approximate weight or volume, if known.
  • A commercial invoice or proforma. You can send this by email or WhatsApp.
  • When the goods will be ready for collection.
  • Your UK delivery postcode.
  • Whether you want DDP, cargo insurance or a pre-shipment inspection.

Goods we ship

We handle legal general cargo, not just furniture. Dangerous goods, including lithium batteries and flammables, must be agreed in advance. Tell us what is in the shipment before requesting collection.

Optional extras

  • Cargo insurance: can be arranged on request and is quoted separately.
  • Consolidation: combine goods from more than one supplier into one shipment, with the scope and costs agreed in your quotation.

Shipping questions

Can you ship goods I bought on Alibaba?

Yes. We can collect from any Chinese supplier, including Alibaba, Made-in-China, 1688 or your own factory. You can use our shipping service whether you have already bought the goods or are still arranging the purchase.

What is DDP?

DDP means delivered duty paid. On request, IK ENG can quote one delivered price that includes UK duty and import VAT, with IK ENG handling them. Confirm this option in your quotation before proceeding.

Should I choose LCL or FCL?

LCL is a shared container or part load, which can suit goods that do not need a full container. FCL is a full container, which can suit a larger shipment. We assess the goods and shipment details to quote the appropriate option.

How long does shipping take?

Sea freight takes about 5 weeks port to port. Allow for supplier collection, consolidation where needed, UK customs clearance and final delivery as well. See our China to UK shipping times guide.

Can you send samples or urgent parts by air?

Yes. We arrange air freight from China to the UK for urgent consignments, samples for approval before a production order, spare parts and replacement components. Air freight costs more than sea freight and is quoted per shipment. Send the goods details and required date so we can confirm suitability before booking.

Do I pay duty and VAT?

If you are the importer, duty and import VAT are shown separately on the quotation. With DDP agreed on request, they are included in one delivered price and handled by IK ENG. See our import duty guide for more context.
