Who a buying trip suits

A China sourcing trip can suit a home buyer planning a house interior, a developer purchasing across properties, or a business buyer choosing products and materials. We plan the visits around your categories, budget and available time.

If you want to buy furniture in China for your home, bring a measured room plan and a clear idea of what each space needs. A Foshan furniture buying trip can form part of the wider itinerary, alongside the other products on your list.

Choose in person and customise the details

See the quality yourself and compare products before choosing. Discuss colour, material, size and specifications with suppliers. We help record your selections and the details that need approval before production, including drawings and samples where required.

Trip support and sourcing fees

We can arrange a visa invitation letter, flight and hotel booking, local transport, food and a professional translator. Trip support is charged separately and depends on your budget, typically about £1,500–£2,000 per person. Your quotation confirms the arrangements and what is included before you book.

Our 5% sourcing fee applies to product value and is separate from the trip-support budget. Products, inspection, warehousing, freight and other order costs are set out in the quotation. The trip-support estimate is not a product or delivered-order budget.

A visa invitation letter does not guarantee a visa or entry. Buyers should check current entry requirements for their passport and circumstances before booking.

What to bring

  • Measured floor plans and room dimensions, checked by your installer.
  • An appliance list with model numbers, sizes and installation drawings.
  • A product and quantity list, plus reference images or a mood board.
  • Your purchasing budget, with provision for the sourcing fee, shipping and delivery.
  • Your delivery postcode, site access details and required timing.

How your order runs after the trip

  1. Record and approve. We bring your selections into an itemised order. You approve specifications, samples or drawings and the commercial terms before production.
  2. Quality control inspection. We organise checks against the agreed scope and specification.
  3. Consolidation and warehousing. We coordinate supplier readiness, receiving, storage and packing for the shipment.
  4. Shipping and customs. We manage shipping, and customs clearance is handled by an authorised UK broker within the agreed order scope. You are the legal importer.
  5. Delivery to your site. We coordinate delivery around the site arrangements recorded for your order.

Product payments are 50% deposit, 30% after factory video confirmation of your finished goods and 20% on delivery. Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items take 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. Your quotation and order confirmation set out the milestones. Trip-support payment arrangements are quoted separately.
