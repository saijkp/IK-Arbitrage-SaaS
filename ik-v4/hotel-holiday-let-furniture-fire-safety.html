Fire Safety for Hotel and Holiday Let Furniture in the UK

Check furniture fire safety before choosing upholstery or mattresses for a hotel or holiday let. Product rules, the operator’s specification and the premises fire risk assessment need to be considered together; this is general guidance, so confirm your position with Trading Standards and a fire safety professional.

Start with the product and its intended use

The Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations cover domestic upholstered furniture supplied in the course of business, including relevant furniture supplied with lets. Mattresses and upholstered bed components also need attention, with product-specific provisions and labelling exceptions. Ask Trading Standards to confirm the scope for each product and intended use; do not assume a domestic furniture label settles a hotel specification.

Permanent labels and the 2025 display-label change

The GOV.UK explanation of the 2025 amendments says that, from 30 October 2025, the display-label provisions were removed. Permanent-label obligations remain. Ask what durable label applies to each item, and check exceptions such as mattress labelling with Trading Standards. An older swing label alone is not enough evidence.

Hotel and contract specifications

Hotel operators often set higher contract fire performance in their own specifications. Agree that written brief with the operator and adviser before asking for factory options; domestic product paperwork may not answer it. Keep the material choices, evidence and approved specification together.

The premises fire risk assessment

The Regulatory Reform (Fire Safety) Order 2005 is relevant to hotels and holiday lets in England and Wales. Furniture choices should be considered in the premises fire risk assessment. Scotland and Northern Ireland have different fire-safety regimes; check the guidance for your location. Use GOV.UK guidance for small paying guest accommodation where suitable, or the sleeping accommodation fire risk assessment guidance for the wider context. Ask a qualified professional which guidance and measures fit the premises.

What to ask a factory for

Request test reports identifying the actual fabric, foam and upholstery combination on the order, with product references that can be traced to the goods. Ask for mattress evidence separately, label artwork and label locations, and confirmation that substitutions need approval. Have your adviser review report relevance and any gaps before production; a generic report for another fabric is not a substitute.

Frequently asked questions

Are display labels still required under the amended furniture regulations?

GOV.UK states that the 2025 amendments removed the display-label provisions from 30 October 2025. Permanent-label obligations remain, with product-specific exceptions to check.

Does domestic furniture paperwork settle a hotel specification?

No. A hotel operator may specify higher contract fire performance. Agree the brief and have the evidence reviewed for the actual product and use.

What reports should a supplier provide?

Ask for reports identifying the actual fabric, foam and upholstery combination ordered, plus mattress evidence where relevant, traceable product references and label details.

Does IK ENG certify furniture fire safety?

No. IK ENG checks that the requested documentation is supplied. Ask Trading Standards or a fire safety professional to review the requirements and evidence.
