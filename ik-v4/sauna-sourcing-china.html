Timber, heaters and options

Timbers commonly offered include thermo-treated spruce or pine, Canadian red cedar, hemlock and aspen. The factory confirms the species and grade in writing for each quote.

Choose an electric heater or a wood-burning stove for a traditional sauna. Electric heater kW must be sized to the cabin volume. We agree the heater and cabin specification before ordering.

  • Glass doors and panoramic windows
  • Roof covering for outdoor units, such as shingles or steel
  • LED lighting, other lighting options and control panels

Electrics and installation in the UK

Electric sauna heaters typically need a dedicated circuit and often a larger electrical supply. Installation must be done by a qualified electrician to BS 7671, which has a dedicated section for locations containing sauna heaters.

Ask for heater and control documentation, including UKCA or CE marking information, wiring diagrams and manuals in English. We check that this documentation is supplied.

For wood-burning stoves, the chimney or flue and clearances need a competent installer; check local rules. Outdoor saunas need a level, solid base. Garden buildings may fall under permitted development, but size, height and distance-to-boundary limits apply: check with your local council.

Installation is not included unless quoted.

Shipping and delivery

Barrel and cabin saunas ship as flat-packed or pre-built panel kits on pallets or in crates. Most single orders go LCL (less than container load); holiday-let parks, spas or resellers ordering several units may fill a container.

Duty depends on how the item is classified: a prefabricated timber building, sauna heater or equipment. We confirm the HS code and duty before you commit. See our guide to UK customs clearance for goods from China.

Delivery is to your address, with offloading arranged separately. Check access for a pallet or crate before ordering.

Inspection before shipping

Inspection checks timber grade, knots and cracks, moisture, finish, doors and glass, heater wiring and labelling, and packing quality against the agreed specification. You receive a pre-shipment photo and video report. Read about inspection and factory visits in China.

Questions about saunas from China

Are Chinese saunas good quality?

Quality varies by factory. Specify the timber species and grade, timber thickness and heater, and arrange a pre-shipment inspection against the agreed specification.

Can I buy just one sauna?

Yes. Single units can ship as LCL (less than container load). Per-unit shipping costs are higher than for multiple units shipped together.

Do I need an electrician?

Yes, electric sauna heaters need installation by a qualified electrician to BS 7671, with a dedicated circuit and a supply sized for the heater.

How long does it take?

Delivery to your UK address typically takes 10–12 weeks. We confirm the timing for each order before you commit.

Can you ship a sauna I found on Alibaba?

Yes. We can ship a sauna you found on Alibaba and arrange shipping from China to the UK. We can also inspect it before it ships.

Looking for a sauna?

Send the sauna type, size, indoor or outdoor use, delivery postcode, budget and timing. We'll come back within 48 hours with factory options and pricing.
