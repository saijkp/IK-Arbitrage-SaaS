Hotel Refurbishment Furniture Packages, Made to Your Room Specification

Furniture packages for independent hotels, guest houses, aparthotels and B&Bs refurbishing roughly 10–40 rooms: one approved room specification repeated across every room, made in Chinese factories, inspected before shipping and delivered room-labelled to the UK.

What a room package includes

Build a package around each room type and its dimensions, rather than assuming every room is identical.

  • Beds and headboards, with mattresses specified separately
  • Bedside tables
  • Wardrobes or open hanging
  • Desks and luggage racks
  • Seating and mirrors
  • Lighting, with positions and connections agreed
  • Soft furnishings by agreement

How it works

Start with the room schedule and drawings. We return factory options within 48 hours of a usable brief; agree a sample or mock-up room before production. After production comes pre-shipment inspection against the approved specification, then room-labelled packing and UK delivery. Confirm installation and offloading scope separately.

Contract specification

Send the operator’s fire performance specifications for upholstery and mattresses before factory selection. Define durability for heavy use, including edge finishes, drawer runners, hinges and cleaning products. Ask for timber species, origin and legality records, and documentation for integrated lighting and USB electrics. We check the documentation is supplied; we do not certify products. Review the evidence with your fire safety professional and electrician. See fire safety for hotel and holiday let furniture.

Repeat orders and spares

We keep the approved specification on file for repeat orders. Record spare fronts, handles and fabric references in the order, and agree quantities to hold on site. Matching finishes on a later batch need reconfirmation and samples; keeping the spec does not guarantee future availability.

Phasing around occupancy

Plan deliveries by floor or blocks of rooms, with a room list for each phase and somewhere dry to store arrivals. Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. Allow time for survey, sampling and installation around closures, and confirm milestones for each phase before ordering. Use the hotel room refurbishment furniture checklist to work back from reopening.

What we need to quote

Send the room count and room types, floor plans or room photos, current specification or mood board, finish level, delivery postcode and access, target dates and budget. Identify any rooms with different layouts and the required installation scope.

Frequently asked questions

Can you repeat one room specification across a hotel?

Yes. We agree a specification for each room type, then repeat it against the room schedule, recording any room-specific dimensions.

Can deliveries be phased around occupied rooms?

Yes. Agree floors or blocks of rooms, storage and delivery dates before ordering. Production and shipping milestones need to fit each phase.

Do you certify fire performance?

No. We check the requested documentation is supplied. The operator should have the specification and evidence reviewed by a fire safety professional.

How long should we allow?

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. Allow additional time for surveying, sampling and installation.

What are the payment terms?

50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading. Shipping, duty and VAT are invoiced separately.
