How to import furniture from China to the UK

Start with a written furniture specification, check the factory and approve samples before committing to production. Plan inspection, freight, customs and site access alongside the order. Buying furniture from China for UK delivery works best when each stage has a named owner and clear approval evidence.

Explore furniture sourcing

1. Define the specification

List each item, quantity, dimensions, materials, finish, upholstery, hardware and intended use. Add room plans, access measurements and reference photographs. Separate essential requirements from optional details. A clear specification makes factory quotes comparable and gives you something concrete to inspect. Read furniture minimum order quantities before fixing the range.

2. Find and check factories

Ask for factory details, product capability, relevant test evidence and photographs of comparable work. Check that the payment beneficiary matches the agreed contracting party. Discuss capacity and whether any work is subcontracted. IK ENG can provide factory options in 48 hours through its furniture sourcing service and offers China buying visits.

3. Approve samples

Request material swatches, finish samples or a product sample appropriate to the order. Check joins, comfort, colour, dimensions and packaging proposals. Photograph and label approvals, then attach them to the order specification. A showroom photograph alone gives you little evidence of the agreed finish.

4. Agree the order and payment stages

Put quantities, specification version, packing, inspection arrangements, payment milestones and change control in writing. State who approves production and how defects are resolved before dispatch. For IK ENG sourcing, the sourcing fee is 5% and payment is 50/30/20; ask for the stages and every cost to be set out in your individual quote.

5. Monitor production and inspect before shipment

Ask for progress photographs tied to your order and confirm the ready date before booking collection. Optional pre-shipment inspection can check the agreed scope before the goods leave China. Define sampling, dimensions, finish, quantities, labels and packing checks in advance, with a written process for rework and approval.

6. Plan LCL or FCL shipping

Use packed carton or crate dimensions and gross weights. LCL shares a container; FCL reserves one for your shipment. Several suppliers can be consolidated. Sea freight is IK ENG’s main service; allow about 5 weeks at sea, with 10–12 weeks door to site for standard furniture and 12–18 weeks for bespoke. Check shipping times against your required date.

7. Prepare UK customs, duty and import VAT

For business imports into Great Britain, arrange a GB EORI and confirm the commodity codes and customs value with your broker. These determine the customs calculation; see GOV.UK: importing goods into the UK. Use the EORI application guide, customs document checklist and furniture duty and VAT guide rather than assuming one duty rate covers every item.

8. Check upholstered furniture fire safety and labels

In-scope domestic upholstered furniture is subject to the Legislation: Furniture and Furnishings (Fire) (Safety) Regulations 1988, as amended flammability requirements. Before ordering, ask for evidence relevant to the actual cover and filling combination, and confirm the product’s scope and intended setting with a competent adviser.

The 2025 amendment removed display or swing-label requirements from 30 October 2025, but did not remove permanent-label obligations. GOV.UK: furniture fire-safety changes in 2025 explains the change. Check the applicable permanent labelling before dispatch; a swing tag alone is insufficient evidence. Our HMO furniture fire-safety guide provides further questions to discuss for furnished rental property.

9. Arrange delivery and installation

Confirm UK postcode, vehicle access, unloading equipment, storage, stairs and lift dimensions before dispatch. Agree who receives and counts cartons, photographs damage and handles installation. Book installers against the confirmed delivery plan and keep a record of missing parts. UK-wide delivery is available through IK ENG; agree the precise delivery and installation scope in your quote.

Frequently asked questions

Can I import a small furniture order from China?

Ask about the factory minimum and packed volume first. IK ENG has no minimum to enquire and can consolidate several suppliers; each job is quoted individually.

Should I approve a sample before production?

A sample helps you check materials, colour, dimensions and finish. Record the approved version and agree how the production goods will be checked against it.

How long should I allow for furniture delivery?

IK ENG planning allowances are 10–12 weeks door to site for standard furniture and 12–18 weeks for bespoke, including about 5 weeks at sea. Confirm the schedule for your order.

Does IK ENG offer shipping without sourcing?

Yes. You can use shipping only if you have already chosen and bought from your suppliers.
