Buying a Garden Room from China: What to Check

Agree the structure, documentation, packing and delivery scope before paying a deposit. Give the drawings to your own builder for review before approving production. IK ENG does not install.

Frame and panel specification

Record frame material, timber species and grade or steel grade, member sizes, panel thicknesses, joints, fixings and anchoring points. Request dimensioned drawings, component lists and available structural calculations for your builder or engineer to assess against site loads and intended use. Agree inspection criteria and photograph key details before packing.

Insulation, U-values and condensation

Specify insulation material and thickness separately for walls, roof and floor. Request factory-stated U-values with their calculation or test basis: clarify whether each figure describes insulation alone or the complete construction. Check the documents against the quoted build-up; do not treat a figure as certification. Ask your designer to review thermal bridges, vapour control, ventilation and condensation risk in the UK climate, especially where a sauna adjoins the room. IK ENG checks that factory documentation is supplied; it does not certify insulation performance or promise year-round use.

Timber treatment and external finishes

Record treatment method, intended exposure, coating system, moisture condition at packing and maintenance instructions. Ask for treatment documentation and details for cut ends, concealed edges and ground clearances. Thermally modified timber and preservative-treated timber are different specifications; have your builder review the proposed materials and weathering details.

Glazing, doors and roof membrane

Confirm glazing dimensions, glass type, safety glazing documentation, frame specification, seals and opening arrangements. Request the stated whole-window U-value and supporting documents where available. For the roof, specify the membrane type, thickness, compatible substrate, seams, flashings, drainage falls and installation instructions. Confirm who supplies gutters, trims, sealants and fixings.

Electrical documentation

List sockets, lighting, controls and any sauna equipment by exact model. Request relevant conformity documents, English manuals, wiring diagrams and supply requirements. IK ENG checks that documentation is supplied and does not certify products. Have a qualified electrician review suitability and the site supply before approval, including installation, testing and any applicable notification.

Crates, containers and inspection

Agree crate dimensions and gross weights, panel supports, moisture protection, glass protection, component numbering and a packing list. Ask how timber packaging documentation will be supplied where applicable. Inspect dimensions, materials, finish, included parts and packing against the agreed specification before loading. Record any corrections and their resolution.

LCL or FCL shipping

Compare LCL and FCL shipping using packed volume, crate lengths, handling constraints and order quantity. A kit may travel as shared-container cargo; a large module may need different transport arrangements. Confirm actual container door clearances and loading method with the freight provider rather than relying on the room footprint.

HS code, duty and UK delivery

Heading 9406 may be relevant to prefabricated buildings, but classification depends on materials and how the goods are presented. Separate components or equipment may differ. Confirm the HS code, full UK commodity code, duty and VAT with your customs broker using the current UK Trade Tariff. Read our UK customs clearance guide. Agree offloading, crane or lifting arrangements and the route from the delivery vehicle to the base before ordering.

Your builder, permissions and installation

Your own builder assesses the ground, base, structural details, assembly, anchoring, weatherproofing and final fit-out. Check with your council about planning and Building Regulations before ordering, especially for sleeping use or hospitality sites. Read GOV.UK guidance for England and our outdoor sauna planning guide. IK ENG does not install or promise Building Regulations compliance.

Payment and quotation scope

Payment is 50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading. Shipping, duty and VAT are invoiced separately. Our sourcing fee is 3–5% of product value; confirm inspection, samples, delivery and offloading scope in the quotation. Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. Confirm milestones before committing.

Frequently asked questions

Can I rely on the factory U-value alone?

No. Ask for the calculation or test basis and the exact wall, roof, floor and glazing build-ups it covers. Your builder or designer should review the figures for the intended use and site. IK ENG checks that documentation is supplied and does not certify performance.

Who installs the garden room?

Your own builder handles the base, assembly, weatherproofing and site work. A qualified electrician handles electrical work. IK ENG does not install.

Is every garden room shipment classified the same way?

No. A prefabricated building, separate components and electrical equipment may be classified differently. Confirm the HS code, full UK commodity code and current duty with your customs broker before committing.

When do I pay the balance?

Payment is 50% deposit to place the order, 50% balance after pre-shipment inspection, before loading.
