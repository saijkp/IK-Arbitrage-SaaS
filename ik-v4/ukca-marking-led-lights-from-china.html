UKCA Marking for LED Lights from China

LED lights from China need evidence for the exact fitting and driver, not just a conformity logo. CE marking remains accepted in Great Britain under the recognised electrical safety, EMC and RoHS routes; UKCA is also available, while Northern Ireland follows different marking rules.

Which GB rules apply to LED luminaires and drivers?

  • Electrical Equipment (Safety) Regulations 2016: apply to equipment designed for 50–1,000 V AC or 75–1,500 V DC, subject to exclusions. Mains-powered luminaires and drivers normally fall within these limits; assess a low-voltage light source and its mains driver separately. Electrical safety guidance.
  • Electromagnetic Compatibility Regulations 2016: address electromagnetic emissions and immunity for equipment in scope, including relevant LED fittings and electronic drivers. EMC guidance.
  • RoHS: the Restriction of the Use of Certain Hazardous Substances in Electrical and Electronic Equipment Regulations 2012 restrict hazardous substances in equipment in scope, including lighting equipment. RoHS guidance.
  • Ecodesign and energy labelling: the GB lighting regulations cover light sources and separate control gear, including those within containing products, subject to definitions and exemptions. Energy labelling applies to light sources in scope; do not assume every luminaire or driver needs the same label. Lighting Products Regulations 2021 and energy information guidance.

CE or UKCA for LED lighting in Great Britain?

CE marking remains accepted in GB for products meeting the recognised EU requirements under the electrical safety, EMC and RoHS regimes; recognition has been extended indefinitely. UKCA remains an available route. Choose the route for the exact product and retain the corresponding declaration and evidence. See GOV.UK guidance on placing UKCA or CE marked products on the GB market.

A mark alone does not establish compliance. Check every applicable regime, including the separate ecodesign and energy-information requirements. IK ENG supplies UKCA-marked electrical goods with a compliance pack; the wider acceptance of CE does not change the need to check the ordered model and its documents.

Northern Ireland has different requirements

Northern Ireland follows relevant EU product rules under the Windsor Framework. UKCA alone is not accepted there: electrical equipment uses CE marking. UKNI is added to CE where a UK body carries out mandatory third-party conformity assessment; it is not a default extra mark for every light. Electrical safety allows manufacturer self-declaration. Read the NI electrical equipment guidance and NI conformity marking guidance. Confirm the destination before ordering.

What to ask the factory for before paying a deposit

  • Signed Declaration of Conformity: UK DoC for UKCA or EU DoC for CE, identifying the exact model, manufacturer, applicable legislation and standards.
  • Full test reports: ask for relevant luminaire safety evidence, for example the applicable EN 60598 series, photobiological safety evidence such as EN 62471, and EMC reports. Request driver evidence for the actual driver model too. Check the standard editions and applicability with a competent compliance specialist using the official designated standards list; these examples are not a universal mandatory test list.
  • Technical file: request the model specification, drawings, component and driver identification, risk assessment and supporting compliance evidence. Ask for RoHS evidence and any required energy label, product information sheet and ecodesign data.
  • Artwork and instructions: obtain photographs of product markings, manufacturer and importer details, model or batch identification, packaging and English safety instructions. Match these to the ordered fitting and driver.

The factory's certificate for a similar fitting is not enough to identify your order. Ask which models and components each report covers, and resolve any discrepancy before approval.

The UK importer's own obligations

For electrical equipment in scope, importers must check the manufacturer's conformity assessment, technical documentation, marking and declaration. Their name or registered trade name or mark and a contact postal address must be on the equipment; packaging or accompanying documents are permitted where marking the equipment is not possible. Do not assume packaging is always an unrestricted alternative for imports from China. The electrical safety importer guidance sets out the conditions.

The same guidance requires the importer to keep the declaration and technical documentation for 10 years after the equipment is placed on the GB market. Importers must also check English instructions and safety information, protect compliance during storage and transport, and act on suspected non-compliance. Ask a competent specialist to review the applicable duties before placing the goods on the market.

Frequently asked questions

Is CE marking still accepted for LED lights in Great Britain?

Yes, for products meeting the recognised EU requirements under the electrical safety, EMC and RoHS regimes, CE recognition in Great Britain has been extended indefinitely. Check all other applicable requirements, including ecodesign and energy labelling, for the exact product.

Can UKCA alone be used for LED lights in Northern Ireland?

No. Electrical equipment placed on the Northern Ireland market needs CE marking. UKNI is added where a UK body carries out mandatory third-party conformity assessment; it is not a default requirement for every light.

Which documents should I request from the LED lighting factory?

Request the signed Declaration of Conformity for the chosen marking route, model-specific safety and EMC reports, technical file, RoHS evidence, marking artwork and English instructions. Ask for applicable ecodesign data and energy-labelling documents too.

Can the importer address always go on the packaging?

No. For electrical equipment imported from China, the electrical safety guidance requires importer identification on the equipment, with packaging or accompanying documents permitted where this is not possible. Confirm the conditions for your exact product.
