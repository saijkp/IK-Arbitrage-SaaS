How long to furnish a serviced apartment?

A serviced apartment furnishing timeline needs approval dates, delivery access and a launch plan as well as factory production. Use weekly planning reviews to manage the stages below, with the order-specific milestones agreed before you commit.

Use published timings as the planning envelope

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. These are the published timings. They are not a promise that a new brief can move straight into production or that site arrival means ready for guests.

For sea freight, allow roughly 5 weeks port to port, as published in our hotel bedroom furniture guide. This is the sea leg within the overall programme, not an extra duration to add automatically to the published to-site timing. Confirm how approvals, production, inspection, customs and delivery fit your actual order.

Week-by-week planning: set review gates, not invented durations

At the opening weekly review, establish the brief and unit mix. At each following weekly review, update approvals, unresolved decisions, forecast milestones and the owner of each action. The sequence below is a planning checklist, not an allocation of a fixed number of weeks to each stage. A stage can span several reviews or overlap another only where the agreed programme allows it.

Brief, factory options and specification

Send unit types, quantities, plans, reference images, delivery postcode and target launch date. Include lift and stair access, unloading limits and who can receive deliveries. Link every product to an apartment and room code; the room-by-room serviced apartment guide helps define the contents.

Our how-it-works page states: “Factory options within 48 hours.” Treat this as the options response, not sample approval or production completion. Compare the proposed scope and pricing, then resolve dimensions, finishes, hardware, compliance evidence and the approval owner.

Samples, model room and production release

Agree which finish samples, product samples or model-room checks are needed. Sample/model-room costs are confirmed in writing before you pay. Record approved drawings and revisions so the factory, inspector and installer work from the same specification. Bespoke timing is tied to approved drawings in the published statement above.

Confirm the production milestones for the selected factories before committing to launch dates. Include Chinese New Year production delays in the discussion: ask each factory about its closure, order cut-off, material availability and restart plan. Do not assume a universal holiday delay.

Inspection, payment and warehouse consolidation

Inspect against the approved schedule, drawings and samples: quantities, dimensions, finishes, hardware, assembly details and packing. Compliance is confirmed per order from factory test evidence: BS 7177 beds/mattresses, permanent fire labels BS 5852 / BS EN 1021 for relevant upholstered items, and UKCA on electrical goods. Close out findings before shipment.

Payment is 50% deposit / 50% after pre-shipment inspection before loading. The sourcing fee is 5% on product value. Keep freight, taxes and site services itemised and confirm their payment dates separately. We consolidate at our warehouse in China; align supplier readiness before fixing the loading plan.

Sea freight, customs and delivery

Confirm the sailing plan and tracking updates, then prepare customs documents with the broker before arrival. Product descriptions, quantities and packing lists must agree with the order. Plan customs clearance and onward delivery as distinct milestones; a port arrival is not a site delivery appointment.

Reconfirm vehicle access, unloading, storage and apartment allocations before dispatch to site. Label cartons by unit, room and item reference. The Ireland hotel guest-room furniture and lighting case study is relevant repeated-room sourcing context when preparing a controlled room schedule.

Installation, snagging and launch

Our own team can carry in, assemble and install as a separately quoted line, or you can use your own fitters. Agree scope, room sequence, fixings, packaging removal and responsibility for specialist connections before delivery. Coordinate electrical, plumbing and other site work with suitable UK trades.

Check each apartment against its inventory and record missing parts, damage and finish snags. Confirm fixes, cleaning, manuals, spare parts and compliance records before operator handover. Set the guest launch after the operator accepts the rooms, with an explicit contingency plan if approvals or shipping milestones move. No fixed installation or snagging duration is given here.

Frequently asked questions

How long to furnish a serviced apartment?

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. Confirm order milestones and allow for brief decisions, installation, snagging and operator handover.

Are factory options the same as production approval?

No. Factory options within 48 hours is the published options response. Final specification, samples and drawings still need approval before the relevant production release.

How long is the sea freight leg?

Allow roughly 5 weeks port to port. Confirm how the sea leg fits the overall order programme, with customs clearance and site delivery tracked separately.

Does Chinese New Year affect the programme?

Discuss factory closure, order cut-off, materials and restart arrangements before agreeing the programme. There is no universal holiday delay to apply to every order.

When can the apartment launch?

Set launch after delivery, installation, snagging, cleaning and operator acceptance. Site arrival alone does not mean the apartment is ready for guests.
