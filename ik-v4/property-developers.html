Furniture Packs for Property Developers

Furniture for property developers, from show homes to new-build, build to rent (BTR), PRS and co-living schemes. We build a costed furniture pack for each unit type, source it from China and plan delivery around your handovers.

Furniture for property developers, planned around handover

Repeated units need a consistent specification and a clear delivery programme. We coordinate factory comparisons, approved samples, inspection and shipping so your site team can work from one agreed furniture schedule.

How we help

  • A fully costed FF&E schedule built from your floor plans — room by room, product names, dimensions, quantities, GBP pricing
  • One consolidated order regardless of scheme size, with staggered delivery timed to your handover programme (Block A in April, Block B in July, for example)
  • Every order inspected before shipping, with a full pre-shipment photo report before the container seals.
  • A compliance pack with BS 7177 evidence for mattresses and beds, permanent fire-label details for relevant upholstered items, and UKCA documents for electrical goods.

What "furniture package" actually means

Not a catalogue you pick items from — a costed schedule built specifically for your scheme: every room type, the exact products, quantities and finish level, priced in GBP, with delivery planned around your handover programme. It's the same document whether the scheme is 10 units or 200.

Send us a floor plan and room types and we'll return a costed, room-by-room FF&E specification within 48 hours — no commitment required to see the numbers.

What we need from your scheme

Send the unit mix, show-home requirements, finish levels, quantities and handover stages. We check repeated products against the same drawing revision and plan factory capacity, sample approvals, spare stock and delivery constraints around your programme.

Budgets per unit

A per-unit budget should state bedroom count, shared-space allowance and exactly what's included. We keep furniture, fitted kitchens, bathrooms, lighting and installation as separate lines, so the headline figure is never an unexplained lump sum. Early planning figures are in our budget planning guide.

How developer furniture schemes work

Start with a per-unit specification: dimensions, quantities, materials, finishes and the budget for each room. We turn your plans into furniture packs for developers with a separate schedule for each layout. A studio, one-bedroom flat and family apartment can share a finish palette without forcing the same furniture into different spaces.

Furniture packages for property developers to repeat

Approve a sample unit or show-home selection before repeating it across the scheme. Record drawing revisions, product references and agreed alternatives so later phases match the first. Show homes can have upgraded pieces while rental units use the same design language at a different specification.

Phased delivery to handover

Group orders by block, floor or handover date. Confirm loading access, storage, room labels and who receives each delivery. We coordinate production and shipping against those stages and review dates when your build programme changes.

Documents, fees and lead times

Frequently asked questions

What is included in furniture packs for developers?

Each pack follows an approved per-unit schedule with product references, dimensions, quantities, materials and finishes. Bedrooms, living and dining spaces are listed separately from kitchens, lighting and installation.

Can you supply different apartment layouts and show homes?

Yes. We prepare separate schedules for each unit type and show-home specification, with a shared finish palette where required.

Can delivery be phased around handovers?

Yes. We plan production, shipping and deliveries by block or handover stage, with access and receiving arrangements confirmed for each phase.

What are your fee and payment terms?

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Pay a 50% deposit to place the order, 30% after factory video confirmation of your finished goods, and 20% on delivery. There is no minimum to enquire.

How early should we send a developer brief?

Allow for approvals before ordering. Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items take 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We return factory options and pricing within 48 hours of your brief.
