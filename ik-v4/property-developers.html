FF&E Sourcing for Property Developers — Built Around Your Handover Programme

New builds, show homes, BTR and PRS schemes. Fully costed FF&E schedules from your floor plans, sourced from China and delivered phased to match your build programme.

The problem

FF&E is rarely the hardest part of a scheme technically — but it's one of the easiest things to let slip the handover date. Dozens or hundreds of near-identical units need consistent spec, multiple blocks need staggered delivery timed to a phased handover, and every week spent chasing UK suppliers for quotes and stock availability is a week the rest of the programme doesn't have.

Sourcing directly from China solves the cost side, but adds a new problem: managing factory relationships, quality control, freight, and customs on top of running the build itself.

How we solve it

  • A fully costed FF&E schedule built from your floor plans — room by room, product names, dimensions, quantities, GBP pricing
  • One consolidated order regardless of scheme size, with staggered delivery timed to your handover programme (Block A in April, Block B in July, for example)
  • Every order inspected before shipping, with a full pre-shipment photo report before the container seals.
  • BS 7177 fire-safety and UKCA documentation for every relevant product, in a compliance pack ready for your property file.

What "furniture package" actually means

Not a catalogue you pick items from — a costed schedule built specifically for your scheme: every room type, the exact products, quantities and finish level, priced in GBP, with delivery planned around your handover programme. It's the same document whether the scheme is 10 units or 200.

Send us a floor plan and room types and we'll return a costed, room-by-room FF&E specification within 48 hours — no commitment required to see the numbers.

What we need from your scheme

Send the unit mix, show-home requirements, finish levels, quantities and handover stages. We check repeated products against the same drawing revision and plan factory capacity, sample approvals, spare stock and delivery constraints around your programme.

Budgets per unit

A per-unit budget should state bedroom count, shared-space allowance and exactly what's included. We keep furniture, fitted kitchens, bathrooms, lighting and installation as separate lines, so the headline figure is never an unexplained lump sum. Early planning figures are in our budget planning guide.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.
