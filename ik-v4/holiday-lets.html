For holiday let owners & operators

FF&E Sourcing for Holiday Lets — Guest-Ready Without the Guesswork

Furniture that photographs well for the listing and survives short-let turnover, sourced at factory pricing whether you're furnishing one property or a portfolio.

The problem

A holiday let has to do two things a standard rental doesn't: look genuinely good in listing photos, and survive far more wear per year than a normal tenancy — every changeover is a new set of guests, and every cancellation or bad review because of tired, mismatched furniture costs bookings directly. Sourcing individual pieces from UK retailers at retail markup eats straight into yield, especially on a single property where there's no scale to negotiate on.

How we solve it

  • No minimum to enquire — we check supplier quantities and shipping practicality for your holiday let
  • Same factory pricing whether it's one property or a growing portfolio
  • Rental-durable material and hardware as the default spec, built for turnover rather than showroom use
  • We review your brief, clarify missing information and agree the sourcing scope and programme before commitment.
Running more than one property? The process scales the same way — one brief per refresh or new listing, consolidated pricing across everything you send us.

Choosing furniture for guest use

We coordinate bedrooms, lounge and dining areas from a practical room list and your references, confirming sofa-bed use, finish durability, cleaning, family use and how replacements will be handled between bookings.

Separate packages

Outdoor furniture, hot tubs, appliances and operational guest equipment are handled as separate packages, each with its own specification and approvals, so your core furniture quote stays clear.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.
