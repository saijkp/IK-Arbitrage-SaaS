EORI number for importing from China

An EORI is the customs identifier used for importing and exporting. A business importing from China into England, Scotland or Wales needs a GB EORI; Northern Ireland has separate XI rules. Confirm who will be the importer before applying or accepting a delivered shipping quote.

What is an EORI number?

EORI stands for Economic Operators Registration and Identification. It identifies a trader for customs activities; it is separate from VAT registration. You can get an EORI without being VAT registered. See GOV.UK: EORI explained. It is also separate from the commodity code, which describes the goods rather than the importer.

Who needs one: GB versus XI

For business imports from China into Great Britain, use a GB EORI. Northern Ireland may require XI; see GOV.UK: importing goods into the UK. GOV.UK: Northern Ireland EORI rules says XI is usually needed to make a declaration in Northern Ireland, among other activities. Exceptions include already holding an EU EORI. Ask a broker to check the route and establishment rules rather than treating GB and XI as interchangeable.

For personal purchases, GOV.UK: who needs an EORI gives an exception where the goods are both not controlled and for personal use only. A small business order is not automatically a personal purchase. If you are not eligible to apply yourself, GOV.UK says to appoint a representative who will need the EORI instead.

How to get an EORI number in the UK

Use the official GOV.UK: applying for an EORI service. For a UK-based applicant, prepare:

  • Your Unique Taxpayer Reference (UTR).
  • Business start date and Standard Industrial Classification (SIC) code.
  • VAT number and registration effective date, if VAT registered.
  • National Insurance number, if applying as an individual or sole trader.

Sign in through the service, creating sign-in details if needed. Non-UK-based businesses do not need a UTR, SIC code or National Insurance number. For XI, check eligibility and apply for GB first; the application page explains the additional VAT and establishment evidence. Save the confirmation and give the broker the correct legal business details before dispatch.

What if the goods arrive without an EORI?

For a business import requiring your EORI, resolve the missing identifier with the broker before shipment so the intended declaration can proceed. Do not use another business’s number as a shortcut. GOV.UK: who should register for an EORI explains that trading without your own EORI can be possible using an indirect representative; this needs a suitable agreed arrangement, not simply a borrowed number.

Practical risks include the broker needing further information while the shipment waits, with possible storage or handling costs. Ask who would pay these in your quote. Prepare the rest of the paperwork using our UK customs clearance guide.

DDP versus importing in your own name

IK ENG offers customer-as-importer arrangements, or DDP on request. Before agreeing DDP, ask who is named as importer, whose EORI is used and who provides the customs records. Do not infer those details from a delivered price. Read DDP shipping explained for the commercial terms and forwarder versus agent for the service roles.

If you are importing in your own name, confirm your EORI and business details with the authorised UK customs broker. Send supplier invoices, goods descriptions and ready dates when requesting IK ENG shipping. For a new furniture project, follow the furniture import steps.

Frequently asked questions

Is an EORI number the same as a VAT number?

No. An EORI is a customs identifier. You do not need to be VAT registered to get one.

Do personal purchases always need an EORI?

GOV.UK says you do not need an EORI for goods that are both not controlled and for personal use only. Check your circumstances before shipping.

Does DDP automatically mean I do not need an EORI?

Do not assume that from the label alone. Confirm the importer and declaration arrangements with the supplier and customs broker before booking.

Can IK ENG arrange customs clearance?

Yes. IK ENG arranges UK customs clearance through an authorised UK customs broker. You can be the importer, or request DDP.
