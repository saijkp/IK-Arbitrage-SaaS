Duty and classification

Building materials carry a wide spread of UK Global Tariff duty — aluminium profiles and extrusions typically around 7.5%, other categories vary from 0% up depending on the exact commodity code. We confirm the exact classification and duty rate before you commit to an order, so the landed cost you're quoted is the landed cost you pay.

For a full build spec spanning multiple material categories, we consolidate everything into one order and one container where possible — reducing per-item freight cost and simplifying customs clearance to a single shipment instead of several.

What we need to quote

Send your product schedule with areas, sizes, thicknesses, finishes, edge details, slip or performance requirements and where each product is going. Wastage is agreed with your installer — a blanket percentage doesn't suit every layout.

Checking quality before it ships

We compare material, grading, dimensions and test evidence, then confirm sample approval, packing and spare or replacement stock. Heavy goods also need realistic unloading and site-access planning, which we build into the delivery.

Specialist products

Structural products, glazing, fire doors and other specialist materials need project-specific technical approval, and we won't offer a generic substitute as compliant. Installation, site works, storage and specialist lifting are separate unless included on your quotation.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.

How do you compare building-material samples?

Record the intended use, dimensions, thickness, finish, installation method and required performance for each product. For tiles and flooring, agree the measured area, cutting allowance and spare stock with your installer. Keep an approved physical sample and batch reference rather than relying on screen colours.

Ask for product-specific test evidence and check it against your designer's specification before approving the order. Coordinate packing and delivery with site storage and the installation sequence. Related schedules include kitchen cabinet sourcing and acoustic panel sourcing.
