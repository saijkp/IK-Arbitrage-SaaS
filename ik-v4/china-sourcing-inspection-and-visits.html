Inspections & buying visits

China Factory Inspections and Buying Visits for UK Buyers

Support for supplier selection, factory checks and procurement coordination — whether you want us on the factory floor for you, or alongside you on a buying trip.

What we offer

  • Supplier identification and side-by-side commercial comparisons
  • Agreed factory visits
  • Inspection photographs and written reports
  • Sample coordination
  • Consolidation and logistics liaison

How an inspection is scoped

Every inspection is scoped before anyone attends: which goods must be ready, whether assembly is needed, the drawing revision and approved samples to check against, how much is sampled, which checks are made, how the report is delivered, and who is responsible for putting faults right. Goods that are already packed can't always be fully checked unless unpacking is arranged in advance — so we agree that up front.

Buying visits to Foshan and Guangzhou

If you'd like to see factories yourself, we can plan a buying visit around your sourcing categories and the appointments available. A Foshan and Guangzhou trip typically spans several days; travel dates, guide and transport fees and factory access are confirmed before you book.

Inspection fees

Inspection days are quoted for the agreed scope and attendance. Flights, hotels, visas, local transport, translation and third-party laboratory testing are included only where stated on your quotation.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.
