Fitted Wardrobe Styles from China

These fitted wardrobe examples help you describe a made-to-order storage brief. They are style references, not stock items ready to fit a room. Start with the IKE references you like, then explain what needs to be stored and how the space will be used. Open dressing walls, enclosed wardrobes and drawer layouts each need dimensions and details that suit your room.

You can specify overall sizes, internal divisions, hanging rails, shelves, drawers, board materials, door finishes and hardware. Record ceiling heights, skirting, sockets, obstructions and door access as well as the width of the wall. Allow for the space needed to open doors and use drawers. Our wardrobes and bespoke joinery sourcing service sets out the information needed to develop your brief.

Agree material and finish samples, then review drawings showing the internal layout, clearances and installation details against checked site measurements. Keep approved samples and drawing revisions as references for production. Inspection should check dimensions, finishes, fittings and packing against the agreed specification; it does not replace accurate measuring before the order. Read our guide to measuring and drawings for fitted wardrobes from China before sending your plans and shortlist.
