Common questions

Everything UK buyers ask
before their first order

What do I need to send?+
A room list, drawings or reference images, delivery postcode, approximate budget and required timing are enough for an initial review.
Is there a minimum order?+
There is no minimum to enquire. Supplier minimum quantities and shipping economics are assessed per product.
How are quality checks arranged?+
Every order is inspected before shipping against the approved specification, with a photo report before the container is sealed. The inspection checks are agreed with you in advance, and samples can be sent to your UK address before bulk production.
Do your products meet UK fire safety and compliance standards?+
Yes. Furniture is supplied to UK fire-safety rules, with BS 7177 compliance on mattresses and beds and permanent fire labels on upholstered items. Electrical goods carry UKCA marking. Test certificates and specifications for each product go into a compliance pack for your project file.
What do you charge?+
Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply. You pay a 50% deposit to place the order and the balance on delivery.
How long does it take?+
Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks door-to-site: 3–5 weeks production, about 5 weeks sea freight, then UK customs and delivery. Bespoke items take 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. Air freight is available for urgent items at a higher cost.
Can I see a completed UK whole-house project?+
We do not currently have a completed UK whole-house project available for viewing. Correctly attributed product or overseas references can be shared where permission allows.
