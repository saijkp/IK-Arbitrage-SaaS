We work from a floor plan, a mood board, or a description as loose as "similar to this but at a lower price point" — no full specification needed to get started.

UK fire safety compliance

Upholstered furniture sold or let in the UK must meet the Furniture and Furnishings (Fire Safety) Regulations, with permanent fire safety labels attached. It is a legal requirement, and HMO and rental property gets checked on it at inspection. We confirm fire-safety compliance (including BS 7177 for mattresses) on every relevant item before it ships, with test certificates in the compliance pack.

How we plan each room

For a living room we confirm the seating layout, sofa footprint, fabric, filling, table materials and the access route — will the sofa actually fit through the door and up the stairs? For bedrooms we confirm bed and mattress size, storage and bedside quantities. We develop coordinated options from your references: an inspiration image is the starting point, and the confirmed specification is what gets ordered.

Standard models or custom

Furniture can be sourced for a single home or consolidated across multiple rooms and properties. Standard factory models are usually more practical for small orders; custom dimensions, fabrics and finishes are confirmed separately. Assembly, positioning and packaging removal are included only where they're priced on your quotation.

Fees, timing & minimum orders

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

No minimum to enquire. Supplier minimums and shipping practicality are checked for each product.
