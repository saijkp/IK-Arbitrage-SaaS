UK fire safety compliance

Upholstered furniture sold or let in the UK must meet the Furniture and Furnishings (Fire Safety) Regulations, with permanent fire safety labels attached — a legal requirement, not optional, and something HMO and rental property gets checked on at inspection. We confirm BS 7177 fire resistance compliance on every upholstered item before it ships, with test certificates included in the compliance pack.
