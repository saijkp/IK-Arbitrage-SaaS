How to Furnish a Holiday Let

Furnish a holiday let around the guests who will use it, the space available and the cleaning routine between stays. Make a room-by-room schedule before ordering so beds, seating, dining places and storage support the same intended guest capacity.

Build a room-by-room buying list

Measure rooms, doorways and access, then mark what you are keeping. Use the list below as a starting point and check quantities against the layout.

  • Bedrooms: beds and mattresses, headboards, bedside surfaces, reading lights, hanging space, drawers and mirrors
  • Living room: enough comfortable seats, practical tables, lighting and media storage
  • Dining area: a table and chairs suited to the intended guest capacity and available circulation
  • Kitchen and utility: usable storage, bins and space for the equipment in your inventory
  • Entrance and bathrooms: coat and shoe storage, luggage space, towel storage and suitable mirrors

Specify durability and easy cleaning

Choose finishes suited to repeated cleaning and ask for care instructions before approval. Consider removable, washable or replaceable covers and accessible spaces beneath furniture. Keep part and fabric references for repairs; ask about replacement availability rather than assuming it.

Match sleeping capacity and bed sizes to the layout

Record actual mattress and bed-frame dimensions, including headboards, before choosing bedding. Check doors, bedside access and walking routes with beds in position. If rooms can switch between twin and double layouts, confirm the beds, connecting arrangements and linen storage support both configurations.

Give guests and cleaners useful storage

Provide hanging and drawer space without crowding the room. Leave somewhere for suitcases and reserve separate lockable owner storage for linen, cleaning materials and spares. Plan how cleaners reach and replenish it between bookings.

Plan outdoor space

Choose furniture for the exposure, cleaning and storage arrangements at the property. Confirm how covers, cushions and loose items are stored during bad weather. For a pergola or other structure, check planning and installation requirements before ordering, and consider routes, drainage and access.

Review fire safety before ordering

Agree the upholstery and mattress specification and evidence with your adviser. Our hotel and holiday let furniture fire safety guide explains the documentation questions and the 2025 display-label change. IK ENG checks documentation is supplied; it does not certify products.

Prepare the property for listing photographs

Finish installation and snagging before photography. Show the real sleeping layouts, dining places, storage and outdoor space guests will receive, with clear room views. Keep the furniture inventory and listing description aligned when layouts change.

Build a budget from a complete schedule

Separate furniture, samples, inspection, shipping, duty, VAT, delivery, offloading and installation. Add the cost of removing old items and holding replacements or linen. Compare quotations against the same quantities and specification, and set a contingency for changes without assuming a headline package includes every task.

Order one package for several properties

Approve a repeat specification, then create a schedule for each property’s dimensions and capacity. Agree property-labelled packing, phased delivery dates and storage for later openings. Send plans and the combined schedule through our holiday let furniture package service.

Frequently asked questions

Where should I start with a holiday-let furniture list?

Start with measured plans, intended guest capacity and a list of items you are keeping. Schedule furniture and quantities room by room.

Should every room use the same bed size?

Choose bed sizes that fit each room and the intended sleeping layout. Record actual bed-frame and mattress dimensions before ordering furniture or linen.

How should I compare package quotes?

Use the same room schedule and specification, then compare the separate lines for goods, inspection, shipping, taxes, delivery and installation.

Can one specification cover several lets?

Yes. Keep a repeat specification and record the dimensions, quantities and layout differences for each property, with delivery phases agreed in advance.
