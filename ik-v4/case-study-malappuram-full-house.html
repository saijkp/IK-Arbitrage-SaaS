Malappuram, Kerala

Case study: full-house supply from China for a private residence in Kerala

IK ENG handled the full-house supply from China for Veepees Mansion, a private residence designed by Arif Associates. The project ran for almost a year and was completed 2020.

The brief

The brief was to source the full-house supply from China for Veepees Mansion, covering doors and windows, the kitchen and wardrobes, lighting, furniture, sanitaryware, tiles, the carved entrance gate and railings.

What we supplied

  • Doors
  • Windows
  • Kitchen
  • Wardrobes
  • Lighting, including chandeliers
  • Furniture
  • Sanitaryware
  • Tiles
  • Carved entrance gate
  • Railings

How we ran it

Sai was personally on the ground for factory visits and coordination, working with our team in China on factory selection.

Our process includes checks against the approved specification before shipping. Our role on this project covered inspection and container shipping coordination.

The challenge

The carved entrance gate was too large to fit into a container the normal way. We turned it on an angle to get it in, and the gate alone took up a whole 40ft high-cube container.

It shipped that way and was installed at the entrance of the residence.
