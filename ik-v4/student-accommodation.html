How it works for student accommodation providers

  1. Control the room-type schedule. Send measured layouts, room and cluster quantities, retained items, postcode, budget and resident arrival constraints. Include unusual and accessible layouts separately.
  2. Approve a model room. Review the proposed bed, mattress, desk, chair and storage with the operator and installer. Check circulation, cleaning, maintenance and product evidence; record amendments before approval.
  3. Release the bulk order. Sign off the final schedule revision, finishes, drawings and accessories. Name the person who can approve substitutions and agree the inspection references.
  4. Pack by room allocation. Agree building, floor, cluster, room, item and carton-sequence labels. Keep the master packing list and separate spare stock from installation stock.
  5. Deliver within the agreed window. Coordinate access, unloading, lifts, receiving contacts and installation sequence with the site team. Allow for delivery checks and correcting shortages before residents arrive.

Specify maintenance and spare stock by item

Record hardware references, cleaning instructions and replacement availability alongside the furniture selection. Student accommodation providers FAQs

When should we approve the student model room?

Approve the final model room and record amendments before bulk manufacture. Check fit, finishes, access, cleaning and maintenance with the operator and installer, then use that approved revision for production and inspection.

Are shared kitchens included in a bedroom price?

No. Shared kitchens, bathrooms and corridor lighting need separate schedules and quotation lines. Confirm what the building contractor supplies so products and installation are not omitted or counted twice.

How should student furniture cartons be labelled?

Agree building, floor, cluster, room, item code and carton sequence. Match fixings to the relevant assembly and keep the master packing list and room allocation sheet aligned with the approved schedule.

How much spare student furniture should we order?

Plan replacement stock by item using wear history, storage capacity and replacement needs. Record component references and keep spare stock separate from installation stock rather than using an arbitrary allowance.

Got rooms to furnish before term starts?

Send your plans, schedule or reference images, delivery postcode, approximate budget and required timing. We'll come back within 48 hours with factory options and pricing.
