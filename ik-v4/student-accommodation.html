FF&E Sourcing for Student Accommodation — Volume, Durability, and a Date That Holds

Hundreds of near-identical rooms, a fixed term-start date that can't move, and furniture that has to survive heavy cyclical use with minimal maintenance in between.

The problem

Student accommodation has a deadline nothing else does: term starts on the date it starts, whether or not the rooms are ready. There's no room for a slipped delivery. At the same time, the per-unit budget is tight across a large number of rooms, and the furniture needs to survive a full academic year of heavy use — sometimes several years of tenants — with minimal maintenance between cycles, not a showroom finish that degrades after one intake.

How we solve it

  • One consolidated order at volume, whether it's dozens or hundreds of rooms, priced consistently across the whole scheme
  • Rental-spec durability as the default assumption, not an upgrade — specified for repeated heavy use, not presentation
  • A confirmed delivery date we stand behind, planned against your term-start deadline with margin built in
  • Staggered delivery for phased or multi-block schemes, so blocks come online in the order you need them
