Louvred, Fixed-Roof or Retractable Pergola?

Choose a pergola roof around the light, shelter and operation you need at the site. Louvred, fixed-roof and retractable systems handle these differently, so compare the exact model’s drainage, load documentation and operating limits before deciding.

Rain protection and drainage

Ask for the model’s stated rain performance and any exclusions for wind-driven rain. Trace the water path through gutters, posts and outlets to the site drain, including cleaning access. A roof does not by itself make an open-sided space dry in every weather condition.

Wind and snow at your site

Give the installer the location, exposure, mounting arrangement and foundation details. Ask a competent designer to compare factory load figures with the site and confirm anchoring. Follow written operating and snow-clearance instructions; do not assume a sensor removes these limits.

Motors, controls and manual operation

Confirm the motor model, controls, sensors and behaviour during power loss. Ask for wiring diagrams, English manuals and relevant marking documentation for the exact equipment. IK ENG checks documentation is supplied; it does not certify products. Arrange professional electrical and installation review.

Maintenance and side screens

Compare access for cleaning gutters, pivots, tracks, seals and fabric, and request a maintenance schedule. Record spare-part references. Side screens change wind exposure and enclosure, so confirm compatibility and operating limits with the supplier and installer rather than adding them later without review.

Homes and hospitality use

For a home, consider daylight into adjoining rooms, views and ease of everyday operation. For a pub, restaurant or hotel, consider staff control, cleaning access, guest circulation, commercial warranty terms and a plan for bad weather. Check planning with your council and agree the full installation specification before ordering.

Frequently asked questions

Which roof lets in the most light?

A retractable roof can open more of the overhead space, while louvres adjust the angle of light. Compare opening dimensions and the remaining frame or stacked roof on the actual design.

Is a closed louvred roof always watertight?

Do not assume that. Ask for the exact model’s rain performance, drainage design and limitations, including wind-driven rain.

Can side screens be added to any pergola?

Confirm compatibility, wind limits, fixings and planning implications with the supplier, installer and council before including screens.

What matters most for hospitality use?

Agree staff operation, cleaning, maintenance access, commercial warranty scope and a bad-weather plan, alongside site-specific load and planning checks.
