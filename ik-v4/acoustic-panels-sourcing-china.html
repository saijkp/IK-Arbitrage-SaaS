Duty and classification

Acoustic foam and panels are typically classified under HS heading 3921 or 3824, carrying UK Global Tariff duty of around 6.5% — but the exact subheading depends on material composition, and getting it wrong either overpays duty or risks a customs query. We confirm the correct HS code and duty rate before you commit to an order.

For commercial and multi-occupancy developments, fire rating matters as much as sound performance. We confirm fire classification (Euroclass rating) and material specification on every acoustic order, with test certificates included in the compliance pack.

What we need to quote acoustic panels

Send wall and ceiling areas, panel dimensions, the substrate you're fixing to, finish and edge details, the fixing method, and the acoustic and fire performance your project requires. A panel's appearance alone doesn't tell you how well it absorbs sound or how it behaves in a fire, so we start from the performance target.

Getting the specification right

Samples let you check colour, veneer and surface quality before bulk production. Performance evidence needs to match the complete build-up as installed — backing, air gap and fixings — not just the panel face, so we check test data against the way the panels will actually go on your walls.

What's priced separately

Installation, battens or backing, adhesives, wastage allowance and substrate preparation are shown as separate lines on your quotation, so you can compare our price with a UK supplier like for like. Every acoustic order is quoted from your own project specification.
