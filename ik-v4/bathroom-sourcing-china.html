Planning each bathroom

Each bathroom is checked separately for usable dimensions, door swing, waste and outlet positions, water services, wall support for wall-hung items, ceiling slopes and access. We identify the exact model and obtain dimensioned drawings before treating any product as suitable for your room.

Water pressure: the check people miss

Many imported taps and showers need higher water pressure than some UK homes provide. A low-pressure gravity system may run poorly with a tap designed for mains pressure. Before ordering, tell us whether you have a combi boiler, an unvented cylinder or a gravity-fed system, so the tap's minimum working pressure can be checked.

UK regulations and test evidence

Anything connected to the water supply must meet the Water Supply (Water Fittings) Regulations in England and Wales, or the equivalent elsewhere in the UK. WRAS approval is one common way to show a product complies. WCs also have a limit on flush volume, so the cistern setting must be confirmed.

The relevant product standard depends on the item, and we ask for evidence that matches the exact model:

  • WC pans and suites: BS EN 997
  • Basins: BS EN 14688
  • Baths: BS EN 14516
  • Shower trays: BS EN 14527
  • Shower enclosures: BS EN 14428, with safety glass to BS EN 12150
  • Taps: BS EN 200

Certificates and test reports are kept in a compliance pack for your order. Your plumber and building control officer remain the final check for the installation itself.

Choosing vanity materials for a bathroom

Bathrooms are harder on furniture than kitchens. Ask how the factory seals exposed edges, the back of the unit and the cut-out around the waste. Plywood and moisture-resistant boards generally suit bathrooms better than standard particleboard. Hinges and runners should also be specified for damp conditions, and a sample of the finish should be approved before production.

Repeating one bathroom across a project

For HMOs, serviced accommodation and developments, one approved schedule can be repeated across rooms. Label each carton by room, confirm replacement parts now and keep model references on file. That keeps the finish consistent and makes later maintenance simpler. For larger projects, phased delivery is checked against supplier capacity and your programme.

Packing and damage protection

Ceramics, stone tops and glass need more packing care than cabinets. Agree the carton and crate specification before production, and label heavy items so they can be handled safely on site. Order spare seals, fixings and shower parts with the main shipment, because a replacement from China can take weeks.

What a pre-shipment inspection should cover

Every order is inspected before shipping, and you receive a pre-shipment photo report. For bathrooms, the checks should compare the finished goods with your approved schedule:

  • Product models, quantities and finishes
  • Chips, cracks and glaze defects on ceramics
  • Glass markings, edges and enclosure parts
  • Tap and fitting finishes, plus any test evidence listed for your order
  • Carton labels, packing and spare parts

Base package and upgrades

We can show a standard fitted-bath package alongside separate upgrades, rather than mixing optional products into the base price. Common questions

Is it worth buying a bathroom from China?

It can be when you need a coordinated specification, several bathrooms or products that are hard to find in the UK, and you have time for manufacture and shipping. For a single urgent replacement, a UK stockist will usually be quicker.

Are Chinese bathroom products legal to install in the UK?

They can be, but it depends on the exact product. Water fittings must meet UK water regulations, and we ask for evidence that matches the model before ordering.

Do you supply WRAS-approved taps?

Tell us if your project requires WRAS approval. We confirm the listing or evidence for the exact product before ordering, rather than assuming it from a factory description.

Can you match a bathroom to my kitchen or wardrobes?

Yes. We can source them together on one schedule, with finishes compared against approved samples.

Do you install bathrooms?

Building works, waterproofing, plumbing, electrics, tiling and installation are separate unless agreed. Have your plumber review the product drawings before you approve them.

How long does a bathroom order take?

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site. Bespoke items typically take 12–18 weeks from approved drawings.

How much does a bathroom from China cost?

As a goods-only planning estimate, a standard bathroom product package typically falls around £1,000–£3,000, and a premium package around £3,000–£8,000 or more. Your quotation is based on the actual schedule.
