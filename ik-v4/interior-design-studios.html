One room furniture & storage sourcing package

Start with one clear room schedule. Keep control of your design while IK ENG coordinates the China sourcing work.

A defined starting scope

One room or repeatable room type, with up to eight distinct hard-furniture or freestanding storage lines. Examples include a dining table, coffee table, sideboard, bedside units or storage cabinet. Quantities and supplier minimums are reviewed before acceptance.

What we need

  • Room plan, usable dimensions and access restrictions
  • Item schedule, quantities, finishes and reference images
  • Delivery postcode, approximate delivered budget and required timing
  • Your design approver and purchasing contact

What we coordinate

  • An agreed product and specification brief
  • Suitable supplier comparisons with material, finish, dimensions, quantities and exclusions identified
  • Itemised costs and packing, transport and import arrangements
  • Drawing or sample approvals where required
  • Agreed inspection checks and reporting before shipment

We seek comparable independent offers before recommendation and identify where suitable offers or evidence remain missing. Initial options are not approval to manufacture.

Your studio stays in control

Your team retains design and specification approval. Site measurement, interior design, installation, electrical work, upholstered seating, fitted joinery and specialist testing are outside this initial package unless separately assessed and agreed.

Fees and timing

Our sourcing fee is 5% of product value, shown on every quotation. Inspection, samples and delivery are listed as separate lines where they apply.

Standard items typically take 10–12 weeks to site; bespoke items 12–18 weeks from approved drawings. We confirm the milestones for your order before you commit.

Start with a scope review

The initial enquiry is free. Send your room schedule and we will assess the fit and explain the proposed sourcing work before you commit.
